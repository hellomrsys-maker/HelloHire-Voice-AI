# Polish Phonological & Orthographic Specification (Text & Sound Layer)

## 1. Consonant Inventory & Sibilant Triad

Polish phonology is world-renowned for its three-way contrast among sibilant series, complex consonant clusters, and contrastive palatalization:

### 1.1 The Three Sibilant Series
| Series | Place of Articulation | Voiceless Fricative | Voiced Fricative | Voiceless Affricate | Voiced Affricate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alveolar (Dental)** | /s, z, t͡s, d͡z/ | **s** (*sen*) | **z** (*ząb*) | **c** (*cena*) | **dz** (*dzwon*) |
| **Retroflex (Post-alveolar)** | /ʂ, ʐ, t͡ʂ, d͡ʐ/ | **sz** (*szum*) | **ż / rz** (*żaba, rzeka*) | **cz** (*czas*) | **dż** (*dżem*) |
| **Alveolo-palatal (Pre-palatal)** | /ɕ, ʑ, t͡ɕ, d͡ʑ/ | **ś / si** (*ślad, siano*) | **ź / zi** (*źródło, zima*) | **ć / ci** (*ćma, cichy*) | **dź / dzi** (*dźwig, dzień*) |

### 1.2 Palatal Orthographic Convention
- Before a vowel: written with the letter **i** (*pies* [pʲɛs], *ciało* [t͡ɕawɔ], *ziemia* [ʑɛmʲa]).
- Before a consonant or word-finally: written with an acute diacritic (**ć, ś, ź, ń**) (*być*, *ktoś*, *brać*, *koń*).

## 2. Nasal Vowels & Neutralization

Polish is the only major Slavic language that preserved phonemic nasal vowels from Common Slavic:
- **ą** (/ɔ̃/): Back mid nasal vowel.
  - Asynchronous pronunciation before stops: pronounced as [ɔm] before labials (*dąb* [dɔmp]), [ɔn] before dentals (*kąt* [kɔnt]), [ɔŋ] before velars (*mąka* [mɔŋka]).
  - Before fricatives: pronounced with nasal glide [ɔw̃] (*wąs* [vɔw̃s]).
- **ę** (/ɛ̃/): Front mid nasal vowel.
  - Follows parallel asynchronous assimilation before stops (*ręka* [rɛŋka]).
  - Word-final position: universally denasalized to pure [ɛ] in standard contemporary speech (*proszę* [ˈprɔʂɛ]).

## 3. Historic Homophones & Spelling Alternations

Standard Polish possesses several pairs of historically distinct phonemes that have merged in pronunciation but remain distinguished orthographically:

### 3.1 *rz* vs. *ż* (Both pronounced [ʐ] or [ʂ])
- **rz**: Reflects historical palatalized /rʲ/. Alternates with **r** in related words or inflectional paradigms:
  - *morze* (sea) -> *morski* (marine)
  - *rower* (bicycle) -> *na rowerze* (on bicycle)
- **ż**: Non-alternating voiced retroflex fricative:
  - *żaba*, *żółty*, *życie*.

### 3.2 *ó* vs. *u* (Both pronounced [u])
- **ó**: Reflects historical long /oː/. Alternates with **o** or **e** in oblique forms:
  - *bóg* (god) -> *boga* (GEN)
  - *stół* (table) -> *stołu* (GEN)
  - *dwór* (court) -> *dworu*
- **u**: Primary letter for etymological /u/: *ucho*, *ulica*, *czuć*.

### 3.3 *ch* vs. *h* (Both pronounced [x])
- **ch**: Alternates with **sz**: *mucha* -> *muszka*.
- **h**: Historical voiced glottal or loanword: *herbata*, *historia*, *honor*.

## 4. Final Devoicing & Regressive Voicing Assimilation

- **Word-Final Devoicing**: All voiced obstruents (/b, d, ɡ, v, z, ʐ, ʑ, d͡z, d͡ʐ, d͡ʑ/) are obligatorily voiceless in word-final codas:
  - *chleb* -> [xlɛp]
  - *bóg* -> [buk]
  - *mąż* -> [mɔw̃ʂ]
  - *obraz* -> [ˈɔbras]
- **Cluster Assimilation**: Obstruent clusters assimilate in voicing regressively to the final consonant:
  - *prośba* [ˈprɔʑba] (voiceless *ś* voiced before voiced *b*).
  - *wstęp* [fstɛmp] (voiced *w* devoiced before voiceless *s*).
