# Polish Syntactic Structure Specification (Sentence Layer)

## 1. Constituent Order & Pragmatic Alignment

### 1.1 Base Word Order & Topic-Focus Dynamics
- Polish exhibits a flexible **SVO** (Subject-Verb-Object) base constituent order, driven primarily by discourse pragmatics (**Topic-Comment / Focus articulation**).
- Old/topical information appears toward the sentence onset (Theme/Topic), while new or salient information occupies the clause coda (Rheme/Focus):
  - Neutral SVO: *Jan czyta książkę.* ("Jan is reading a book.")
  - Object Topicalization (OVS): *Książkę czyta Jan.* ("It is Jan who is reading the book.")
  - Verb Initial (VSO): *Czyta Jan tę książkę.* (Narrative/emphatic).

### 1.2 Pro-Drop (Pronoun Dropping) Invariant
- Polish is a canonical **pro-drop** language. Subject pronouns (*ja, ty, on, ona, ono, my, wy, oni, one*) are omitted by default because person, number, and gender are explicitly coded in finite verb conjugations:
  - *Czytam książkę.* ("I am reading a book." - 1SG present, pronoun *ja* dropped).
  - *Poszła do domu.* ("She went home." - 3SG feminine past, pronoun *ona* dropped).
- Inclusion of overt subject pronouns serves emphatic contrast or disambiguation:
  - *To ja to zrobiłem, a nie ty.* ("It was I who did it, not you.")

## 2. Syntactic Invariants & Case Government

### 2.1 The Genitive of Negation (*Dopełniacz Negacji*)
An absolute, non-negotiable rule of Polish morphosyntax:
- Whenever a transitive verb taking a direct object in the **Accusative (Biernik)** is negated with **nie**, the direct object **MUST shift to the Genitive (Dopełniacz)**:
  - Affirmative (Accusative): *Mam czas.* (Acc: *czas*) -> "I have time."
  - Negative (Genitive): *Nie mam **czasu**.* (Gen: *czasu*, never \*nie mam czas).
  - Affirmative (Accusative): *Kupuję chleb.* -> "I buy bread."
  - Negative (Genitive): *Nie kupuję **chleba**.* (Gen: *chleba*).
  - Affirmative (Accusative): *Czytam książkę.* -> "I read a book."
  - Negative (Genitive): *Nie czytam **książki**.* (Gen: *książki*).

### 2.2 Double / Concordial Negation (*Podwójne Zaprzeczenie*)
Polish requires negative concord across all negative pronouns and the verbal negator *nie*:
- *Nikt nic nie wie.* (lit. "Nobody nothing not knows" -> "Nobody knows anything.")
- *Nigdy nikomu nic nie mów.* (lit. "Never to-nobody nothing not say" -> "Never tell anything to anyone.")

### 2.3 Reflexive Particle *się* Positioning
The reflexive pronoun/marker *się*:
- Functions as an enclitic and tends toward second syntactic position (Wackernagel position).
- In high literary style, *się* must not appear at the absolute start of a sentence (\*Się myję is strictly incorrect; *Myję się* is standard) nor at the absolute end if monosyllabic words precede it.

### 2.4 Prepositional Case Government
Prepositions in Polish govern specific syntactic cases:
- **Genitive**: *do* (to/into), *od* (from), *z* (out of), *bez* (without), *dla* (for), *u* (at the house of).
- **Dative**: *dzięki* (thanks to), *ku* (towards), *przeciwko* (against).
- **Accusative**: Directional *w / na / pod / za* (motion into/onto), *przez* (through/by).
- **Instrumental**: *z* (together with), *pod / nad / przed / za / między* (static location below/above/in front of/behind/between).
- **Locative**: Static *w* (inside), *na* (on), *o* (about), *po* (after/along), *przy* (near/by).
