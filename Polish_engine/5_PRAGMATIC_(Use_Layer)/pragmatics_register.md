# Polish Pragmatics & Register Specification (Use Layer)

## 1. Social Deixis: The *Pan / Pani* Honorific Address System

Polish social etiquette strictly segregates familiar, intimate relations from professional or formal interactions through a unique grammatical address paradigm:

### 1.1 The Third-Person Honorific Rule
Unlike French (*vous*) or German (*Sie*) which employ second or third person plural pronouns, Polish employs **nominal honorific titles that govern 3rd-person verb agreement**:

| Addressee | Honorific Form | Grammatical Agreement | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Formal Male** | *Pan* | 3rd person singular masculine | *Czy **Pan wie**, gdzie to jest?* ("Do you know where it is, Sir?") |
| **Formal Female** | *Pani* | 3rd person singular feminine | *Czy **Pani przeczytała** raport?* ("Have you read the report, Ma'am?") |
| **Formal Group (Mixed / Male)** | *Państwo* | 3rd person plural virile | *Czy **Państwo chcą** kawy?* ("Would you like some coffee?") |
| **Formal Group (All-Female)** | *Panie* | 3rd person plural non-virile | *Czy **Panie czekają** na kogoś?* ("Are you ladies waiting for someone?") |
| **Informal (Friend / Peer)** | *ty* | 2nd person singular | *Czy **wiesz**, gdzie to jest?* ("Do you know where it is?") |

### 1.2 Grammatical Invariant
Directly addressing an acquaintance, stranger, or professional superior as *ty* without an explicit mutual agreement (*przejście na ty* / *bruderszaft*) is a severe pragmatic faux pas.

## 2. Epistolary Conventions & Business Correspondence

### 2.1 Formal Salutations (Obligatory Vocative Case)
In formal written communication, titles and names MUST be inflected in the **Vocative (Wołacz)**:
- *Szanowny Panie Profesorze,* (To a male professor; *profesor* -> vocative *profesorze*).
- *Szanowna Pani Dyrektor,* (To a female director).
- *Szanowny Panie Janie,* (Formal yet warm).
- *Szanowni Państwo,* (To an organization, committee, or unspecified audience).

### 2.2 Formal Valedictions
- *Z poważaniem,* (Standard formal business/official closure).
- *Z wyrazami szacunku,* (High formal, diplomatic, or judicial sign-off).
- *Łączę wyrazy szacunku,* (Elevated traditional style).

### 2.3 Informal Correspondence
- Salutations: *Drogi Michale,* (Vocative of Michał), *Droga Aniu,* (Vocative of Ania), *Cześć Tomek / Tomku!*
- Valedictions: *Pozdrawiam serdecznie,*, *Ściskam,*, *Trzymaj się!*

## 3. Pragmatic Discourse Markers & Particles

- **przecież**: Appeals to shared knowledge or self-evident reality (*Przecież wiesz, że to prawda* - "You know it's true, after all").
- **chyba**: Epistemic hedge indicating probability (*Chyba masz rację* - "I suppose you might be right").
- **no**: Ubiquitous colloquial conversational marker (*No jasne* - "Well obviously", *No weź* - "Come on").
- **właśnie**: Confirms exactness or topical focus (*Właśnie o tym mówiłem* - "That is precisely what I was talking about").
