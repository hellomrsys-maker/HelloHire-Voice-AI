# Polish Semantic Representation & Idiomatic Specification (Meaning Layer)

## 1. Untranslatable Cultural Semantics

Polish cultural semantics embodies historical resilience, social resourcefulness, and emotional depth:

### 1.1 *Załatwić* & *Kombinować*
- **Załatwić**: Untranslatable pragmatic verb signifying getting a complex matter resolved, arranged, or settled through interpersonal resourcefulness, informal channels, or skillful negotiation (*Muszę to załatwić w urzędzie* - "I need to get this sorted at the office").
- **Kombinować**: Denotes creative problem-solving, scheming, or finding ingenious workarounds to circumvent rigid bureaucratic or practical obstacles.

### 1.2 *Tęsknota*
- Conveys profound, existential yearning, bittersweet nostalgia, and longing for an absent homeland, person, or time (paralleling Portuguese *saudade* or Romanian *dor*).

## 2. Canonical Polish Idioms & Metaphorical Domains

| Polish Idiom | Literal Translation | Figurative Semantic Meaning | Context / Register |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nie mój cyrk, nie moje małpy** | "Not my circus, not my monkeys" | Not my problem; not my concern | Colloquial / Expressive |
| **Wiercić komuś dziurę w brzuchu** | "To drill a hole in someone's belly" | To pester or badger someone relentlessly | Conversational |
| **Rzucać grochem o ścianę** | "To throw dried peas against a wall" | Talking to a brick wall; futile advice | Colloquial / Resigned |
| **Mieć węża w kieszeni** | "To have a snake in one's pocket" | To be stingy, tight-fisted | Evaluative / Humorous |
| **Urwać się z choinki** | "To drop off the Christmas tree" | To be completely out of touch, clueless | Colloquial / Critical |
| **Czuć miętę do kogoś** | "To feel mint towards someone" | To have a romantic crush or attraction | Informal / Playful |
| **Dzielić skórę na niedźwiedziu** | "To divide the skin on the bear" | Counting chickens before they hatch | Idiomatic / Cautionary |
| **Z deszczu pod rynnę** | "From the rain under the gutter" | Out of the frying pan into the fire | Classic proverb |

## 3. Verbal Prefixation Semantics

Prefixation systematically converts imperfective stems into telic perfective actions with spatial or qualitative shifts:
- **prze-**: Transgression, overdoing, or duration from start to finish (*pisać* "write" -> *przepisać* "rewrite / copy out"; *czytać* -> *przeczytać* "read through").
- **za-**: Initiation or completion (*dzwonić* "ring" -> *zadzwonić* "make a call"; *płacić* -> *zapłacić* "pay in full").
- **do-**: Reaching an endpoint or addition (*pisać* -> *dopisać* "add to a text"; *jechać* -> *dojechać* "arrive at destination").
- **od-**: Separation, reversal, or replying (*dać* "give" -> *oddać* "give back"; *powiedzieć* -> *odpowiedzieć* "reply").
