# Polish Morphological Analysis Specification (Word Layer)

## 1. Nominal Declension & The 7 Grammatical Cases

Polish possesses a rich, highly synthetic fusional nominal morphology governed by 7 grammatical cases across singular and plural numbers:

| Case # | Polish Name | Latin / English | Typical Question | Primary Syntactic Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Mianownik** | Nominative (NOM) | *Kto? Co?* | Subject of finite clause, predicate nominal with *to* |
| **2** | **Dopełniacz** | Genitive (GEN) | *Kogo? Czego?* | Possession, Genitive of Negation, quantifiers (*pięć kotów*), prepositions (*do, od, bez*) |
| **3** | **Celownik** | Dative (DAT) | *Komu? Czemu?* | Indirect object, experiencer, prepositions (*dzięki, ku*) |
| **4** | **Biernik** | Accusative (ACC) | *Kogo? Co?* | Direct object of affirmative transitive verbs, directional prepositions (*przez, w, na*) |
| **5** | **Narzędnik** | Instrumental (INST) | *Z kim? Z czym?* | Instrument/means, predicate nominal with *być* (*Jestem lekarzem*), accompaniment (*z*) |
| **6** | **Miejscownik** | Locative (LOC) | *O kim? O czym?* | Location / topic; obligatorily governed by prepositions (*w, na, o, po, przy*) |
| **7** | **Wołacz** | Vocative (VOC) | *O!* | Direct address / invocations (*Panie Janie!*, *Boże!*, *Mamo!*) |

## 2. Gender Classification & Animacy Hierarchy

In singular, Polish distinguishes 3 genders, with masculine sub-partitioned by animacy:
1. **Masculine Inanimate (*Męskorzeczowy*)**: ACC = NOM (*Stół stoi. Widzę stół.*)
2. **Masculine Animate (*Męskozwierzęcy*)**: ACC = GEN (*Pies biegnie. Widzę psa.*)
3. **Masculine Personal (*Męskoosobowy / Virile*)**: ACC = GEN (*Lekarz idzie. Widzę lekarza.*)
4. **Feminine (*Żeński*)**: Typified by *-a* or palatal coda (*kobieta, noc*).
5. **Neuter (*Nijaki*)**: Typified by *-o, -e, -ę, -um* (*okno, morze, imię, muzeum*).

In plural, Polish collapses into a dual partition:
- **Virile (*Męskoosobowy*)**: Groups containing at least one male human (*panowie, studenci, Polacy*).
- **Non-Virile (*Niemęskoosobowy*)**: All other groups (women, children, animals, inanimate objects: *kobiety, psy, stoły*).

## 3. Verbal Aspect & Conjugation Classes

Every Polish verb root belongs to either the **Imperfective (*Niedokonany*)** or **Perfective (*Dokonany*)** aspect:

### 3.1 Aspectual Derivation Strategies
1. **Prefixation (Prefiksacja)**:
   - *pisać* (impf, to write) -> *napisać* (pf, to finish writing / write down)
   - *robić* (impf, to do/make) -> *zrobić* (pf, to complete/make)
   - *czytać* (impf, to read) -> *przeczytać* (pf, to read through)
2. **Suffixation & Stem Alternation (Sufiksacja)**:
   - *kupować* (impf) -> *kupić* (pf, to buy)
   - *otwierać* (impf) -> *otworzyć* (pf, to open)
   - *dawać* (impf) -> *dać* (pf, to give)
3. **Suppletion (Supletywizm)**:
   - *brać* (impf, take) -> *wziąć* (pf)
   - *widzieć* (impf, see) -> *zobaczyć* (pf)

### 3.2 Tense & Aspect Alignment
- **Present Tense**: Exists ONLY for imperfective verbs (*czytam* = "I am reading").
- **Future Tense**:
  - Imperfective Compound Future: *będę czytać* / *będę czytał* ("I will be reading").
  - Perfective Simple Future: *przeczytam* ("I will read/finish reading").
- **Past Tense**: Marked by *-ł-* suffix with overt gender and person endings (*czytałem* [1SG M], *czytałam* [1SG F]).

## 4. Morphophonological Alternations

- **Mobile 'e' (*Ruchome e*)**: Unstressed /ɛ/ elides in oblique cases:
  - *pies* (dog.NOM) -> *psa* (dog.GEN)
  - *dzień* (day.NOM) -> *dnia* (day.GEN)
  - *kotek* (kitten.NOM) -> *kotka* (kitten.GEN)
- **Consonant Palatalization / Softening**:
  - *k* -> *cz* (*ręka* -> *rączka*)
  - *t* -> *ci/ć* (*brat* -> *bracie*)
  - *r* -> *rz* (*rower* -> *na rowerze*)
