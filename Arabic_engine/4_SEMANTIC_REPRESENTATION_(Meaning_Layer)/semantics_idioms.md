# Arabic Engine — Layer 4: Semantic Representation (Meaning Layer)

## 1. Triconsonantal Root Conceptual Spaces

In Semitic semantics, every triconsonantal root anchors an expansive lexical-conceptual matrix:

| Root | Core Semantic Field | Derived Instantiations |
|------|---------------------|------------------------|
| $\sqrt{s-l-m}$ | Peace, Safety, Wholeness, Surrender | *salām* (peace), *islām* (submission), *salāma* (safety), *aslama* (converted), *istalama* (received), *taslīm* (delivery) |
| $\sqrt{'-l-m}$ | Knowledge, Cognition, Marking | *'ilm* (science/knowledge), *'ālim* (scholar), *mu'allim* (teacher), *ta'allama* (learned), *ma'lūm* (known), *i'lām* (media/information) |
| $\sqrt{'-m-l}$ | Work, Action, Operation | *'amal* (work), *'āmil* (worker/factor), *isti'māl* (usage), *mu'āmala* (transaction/treatment), *ma'mal* (laboratory) |
| $\sqrt{q-r-'}$ | Recitation, Reading, Gathering | *qara'a* (read), *qirā'a* (reading), *qur'ān* (recitation/Quran), *qāri'* (reader) |

## 2. Negation Semantics Across Tenses & Moods

Arabic employs specific negative particles coupled with verbal moods to specify temporal and aspectual negation:

| Particle | Governed Mood / Form | Meaning / Time Horizon | Example |
|----------|----------------------|------------------------|---------|
| **لَا (*lā*)** | Present Indicative (Marfū') | General / Habitual Present | *lā yaktubu* (لا يَكْتُبُ - He does not write) |
| **لَمْ (*lam*)** | Jussive Mood (Majzūm) | Past Negative ("did not") | *lam yaktub* (لَمْ يَكْتُبْ - He did not write) |
| **لَنْ (*lan*)** | Subjunctive Mood (Manṣūb) | Emphatic Future ("will not") | *lan yaktuba* (لَنْ يَكْتُبَ - He will not write) |
| **مَا (*mā*)** | Past (Māḍī) / Nominal | Past fact / Present state | *mā kataba* (مَا كَتَبَ - He did not write) |
| **لَيْسَ (*laysa*)** | Takes Accusative Predicate | Equational / Copular ("is not") | *laysa al-jawwu bāridan* (لَيْسَ الجَوُّ بَارِداً) |

## 3. Authentic Idiomatic Expressions (التَّعْبِيرَاتُ الاِصْطِلَاحِيَّةُ)

Modern Standard Arabic and classical prose frequently utilize formulaic semantic units:
- **على الرأس والعين (*'alā ar-ra'si wa-l-'ayn*)**: With utmost pleasure / At your service.
- **بفضل (*bi-faḍli*)**: By virtue of / Thanks to (positive attribution).
- **من جهة أخرى (*min jihatin 'ukhrā*)**: On the other hand.
- **قلباً وقالباً (*qalban wa-qāliban*)**: Heart and soul / In form and spirit.
- **في غاية الأهمية (*fī ghāyati al-'ahammiyya*)**: Of paramount importance.
- **لا سيما (*lā siyyamā*)**: Especially / In particular.
