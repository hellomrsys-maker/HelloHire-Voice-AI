# Layer 8: Evolution & Geographic Variation — Spanish Engine

## 1. Major Dialect Macro-Zones
1. **Peninsular Spanish (Spain / Castellano)**:
   - Features *distinción* (`/θ/` vs `/s/`), *vosotros/as* for informal plural, and varying degrees of *leísmo* (use of *le* for masculine animate direct objects).
2. **Mexican & Central American Spanish**:
   - Predominance of seseo, conservative consonantism in the Mexican highlands, extensive diminutive usage, *ustedes* for all plurals.
3. **Caribbean Spanish (Cuba, Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, coastal areas)**:
   - S-aspiration and deletion in syllable codas (*eh-pañol*), lambdaism (lateralization of /r/ to /l/: *puerto* $\to$ *puelto*), overt subject pronoun retention.
4. **Andean Spanish (Highland Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia)**:
   - Conservative vowels, retention of coda consonants, distinct polite ustedeo, substratum influence from Quechua and Aymara.
5. **Rioplatense Spanish (Argentina, Uruguay)**:
   - Systematic *voseo* (*vos cantás, vos tenés*), *žeísmo / šeísmo* (pronouncing *ll* and *y* as `/ʒ/` or `/ʃ/`), Italianate intonational contours.
6. **Chilean Spanish**:
   - Distinctive verbal voseo (*vos cantái, vos sabí*), aspiration of final consonants, specialized discourse particles (*po*, *cachai*).
