# Layer 5: Pragmatic Layer (Use Layer) — Spanish Engine

## 1. Tri-Partite Pronominal Address System
Spanish pragmatics is anchored in distance, hierarchy, solidarity, and geographic provenance:

| Register Level | Pronoun | Verb Agreement | Context & Usage | Geographic Distribution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tuteo (Informal)** | *Tú* | 2nd person singular (*hablas, tienes, vienes*) | Peers, friends, family, children | Pan-Hispanic (universal) |
| **Voseo (Informal / Intimate)**| *Vos* | 2nd person monophthongized (*hablás, tenés, venís*) | Intimate peers, local solidarity | Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay, Central America, parts of Colombia |
| **Ustedeo (Formal / Deference)**| *Usted* | **3rd person singular** (*habla, tiene, viene*) | Elders, professional superiors, clients, strangers | Pan-Hispanic (formal); Colombia/Costa Rica (widespread) |
| **Vosotros (Informal Plural)** | *Vosotros/as*| 2nd person plural (*habláis, tenéis*) | Multiple informal addressees | **Spain exclusively** |
| **Ustedes (Universal Plural)** | *Ustedes* | **3rd person plural** (*hablan, tienen*) | All plural addressees in Latin America; formal in Spain | Latin America (universal); Spain (formal only) |

## 2. Politeness Strategies & Formulas of Courtesy (*Fórmulas de Cortesía*)
- **Formal Epistolary & Business Protocols**:
  - Salutations: *Estimado/a señor/a [Apellido]*, *Apreciado/a doctor/a [Apellido]*.
  - Body Framing: *Me dirijo a usted con el propósito de...*, *Agradezco de antemano su atención...*
  - Closings: *Atentamente*, *Le saluda cordialmente*, *Quedo a su entera disposición*.
- **Informal Communications**:
  - Salutations: *Hola [Nombre]*, *¡Qué tal!*, *Querido/a [Nombre]*.
  - Closings: *Un abrazo*, *Nos vemos pronto*, *Saludos*.

---

## 3. Pragmatic Attenuation (*Atenuación & Mitigación*)
To soften face-threatening acts (requests, refusals, commands):
- **Conditional of Courtesy**:
  - *¿Podría indicarme la hora?* (more polite than *¿Puede...?*).
  - *Desearía solicitar una reunión.*
- **Imperfect of Courtesy**:
  - *Quería preguntarle una duda.* (past imperfect softens present demand).
- **Diminutives as Pragmatic Softeners**:
  - *Espéreme un momentito.*
  - *¿Me da un vasito de agua?*
