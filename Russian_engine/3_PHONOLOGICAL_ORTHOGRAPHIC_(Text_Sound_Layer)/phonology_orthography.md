# Layer 3: Phonological & Orthographic Layer - Russian Engine (Русский язык)

## 1. Russian Cyrillic Orthography (Русский алфавит)
The standard Russian alphabet consists of 33 letters:
- **10 Vowels**:
  - Hard indicators: А, Э, Ы, О, У
  - Soft / Iotated indicators: Я [ja], Е [je], И [i], Ё [jo], Ю [ju]
- **21 Consonants**: Б, В, Г, Д, Ж, З, Й, К, Л, М, Н, П, Р, С, Т, Ф, Х, Ц, Ч, Ш, Щ
- **2 Orthographic Signs**:
  - **Soft Sign (Ь)**: Indicates palatalization of preceding consonant (*мать* [matʲ]) or separating iotated vowel (*семья* [sʲɪmˈja]).
  - **Hard Sign (Ъ)**: Separates prefix consonant from following iotated vowel preventing palatalization (*объект* [ɐbˈjekt]).

---

## 2. Palatalization (Мягкость и твёрдость согласных)
Most Russian consonants exist in phonemically paired **Hard (velarized/plain)** and **Soft (palatalized)** forms:
- Pairs: /b/-/bʲ/, /p/-/pʲ/, /d/-/dʲ/, /t/-/tʲ/, /g/-/gʲ/, /k/-/kʲ/, /v/-/vʲ/, /f/-/fʲ/, /z/-/zʲ/, /s/-/sʲ/, /m/-/mʲ/, /n/-/nʲ/, /l/-/lʲ/, /r/-/rʲ/.
- Always Hard: Ж [ʐ], Ш [ʂ], Ц [ts].
- Always Soft: Ч [tɕ], Щ [ɕː], Й [j].

---

## 3. Vowel Reduction Dynamics (Редукция гласных)
Russian vowel qualities depend strictly on lexical dynamic stress (ударение):
1. **Akan'ye (Аканье)**:
   - Unstressed *О* and *А* merge after hard consonants into near-open central vowel [ɐ] in the immediate pre-tonic syllable:
   - *молоко* $\to$ [məlɐˈko]
   - *вода* $\to$ [vɐˈda]
2. **Ikan'ye (Иканье)**:
   - Unstressed *Е*, *Я*, and *И* merge after soft consonants into near-close front vowel [ɪ]:
   - *часы* $\to$ [tɕɪˈsɨ]
   - *река* $\to$ [rʲɪˈka]

---

## 4. Consonantal Assimilation & Final Devoicing
1. **Final Devoicing (Оглушение на конце слова)**:
   - Voiced obstruents (б, в, г, д, ж, з) become voiceless (п, ф, к, т, ш, с) at the end of a word:
   - *хлеб* [xlʲep], *город* [ˈɡorət], *друг* [druk].
2. **Regressive Voicing Assimilation**:
   - Voiceless becomes voiced before voiced stops/fricatives: *сделать* [ˈzdʲelətʲ].
   - Voiced becomes voiceless before voiceless consonants: *лодка* [ˈlotkə], *вчера* [ftɕɪˈra].
