# Layer 5: Pragmatics, Register & Sociolinguistic Dynamics (Russian Language Engine)

## 1. The T-V Distinction (*Ты* vs *Вы*) & Honorific Dynamics

Russian grammar enforces a strictly stratified binary distinction in second-person address, governing interpersonal distance, hierarchy, and context:

### 1.1 Second-Person Address Matrix

| Form | Number | Grammatical Agreement | Pragmatic Sphere | Sociolinguistic Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ты** | Singular | Singular 2nd person verb / sing. adj. | Intimate / Informal | Family, close friends, children (<14), peer-group casual banter. |
| **Вы** (Plural) | Plural | Plural 2nd person verb / plur. adj. | General Plural | Addressing two or more persons regardless of social rank. |
| **Вы / Вам / Вас** (Honorific) | Singular referent | Plural verb / Plural or Short Adj. | Formal / Polite | Unfamiliar adults, professional superiors, clients, teachers, doctors. |
| **Вы** (Capitalized in writing) | Singular referent | Plural verb agreement | Epistolary Deference | Direct official letters, formal business correspondence, personal dedication. |

### 1.2 Agreement Rules with Polite *Вы*
When addressing a single person respectfully as *Вы*:
1. **Verb Agreement**: Verbs MUST remain in the grammatical **plural**:
   - *Вы пришли вовремя.* (Never: \**Вы пришёл* or \**Вы пришла*).
2. **Predicate Adjective Agreement**:
   - **Long Adjective / Participle**: Usually plural: *Вы сегодня такой внимательный / такие внимательные.*
   - **Short Adjective**: Predominantly plural: *Вы правы* (You are right), *Вы готовы?* (Are you ready?).

## 2. The East Slavic Anthropomastic System (Naming Hierarchy)

Russian names comprise three components: **Имя** (Given Name), **Отчество** (Patronymic), and **Фамилия** (Surname). The combination chosen signals exact social distance:

```
[Family Name / Фамилия]  <-- Official, bureaucratic records, police, indexing
        ^
        |
[Given Name + Patronymic / Имя Отчество]  <-- Standard respectful adult address (corporate, medical, academic)
        ^
        |
[Full Given Name / Полное имя]  <-- Professional peers, semi-formal
        ^
        |
[Hypocoristic / Уменьшительное]  <-- Close friends, relatives, children (e.g., Александр -> Саша)
        ^
        |
[Endearment / Уменьшительно-ласкательное]  <-- Affectionate (Сашенька, Сашуля)
```

### 2.1 Patronymic Morphology
Patronymics are formed systematically from the father's given name:
- **Masculine**: *-ович / -евич / -ич* (e.g., *Владимир* → *Владимирович*; *Сергей* → *Сергеевич*; *Илья* → *Ильич*).
- **Feminine**: *-овна / -евна / -ична* (e.g., *Владимир* → *Владимировна*; *Сергей* → *Сергеевна*; *Илья* → *Ильинична*).

### 2.2 Functional Pragmatic Address Styles
| Target Addressee | Proper Address Form | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Executive / Professor / Client** | *Уважаемый(ая) + Имя + Отчество* | *Уважаемый Виктор Сергеевич!* |
| **Peer Colleague (Formal)** | *Коллега* or *Имя* + *Вы* | *Алексей, передайте, пожалуйста, файл.* |
| **Peer Colleague (Informal)** | *Имя* (Diminutive) + *Ты* | *Лёша, пойдём на обед.* |
| **Bureaucratic / Legal** | *Фамилия + Инициалы* or Full Trio | *Иванов Пётр Алексеевич* |

## 3. Stylistic Functional Registers (Функциональные стили)

1. **Официально-деловой (Official-Business / Administrative)**
   - Characterized by high density of de-verbal nouns (*рассмотрение, исполнение, аннулирование*), passive voice, standardized legal cliches (*во избежание, принять меры, довести до сведения*), and complete absence of emotional coloring.

2. **Научный (Scientific / Academic)**
   - High frequency of abstract terminology, passive and reflexive constructions (*эксперимент проводится, доказано, что...*), 1st person plural authorial "we" (*мы полагаем, в нашем исследовании*), logical connective particles (*следовательно, во-первых, таким образом*).

3. **Публицистический (Journalistic / Media)**
   - Combines persuasive rhetoric, expressive evaluative vocabulary (*беспрецедентный, эпохальный, кризис*), rhetorical questions, and syntactically balanced periodic sentences.

4. **Разговорный (Colloquial / Everyday Speech)**
   - Ellipsis (dropping copula, auxiliary verbs, or predictable arguments: *Ты куда? — В кино.*), expressive diminutive suffixes (*водичка, минуточка*), conversational particles (*да, ну, вот, ведь, же*), and variable word order dictated by communicative dynamism.

## 4. Epistolary Standards & Formulas

### 4.1 Salutation Formulas
- **Highly Formal / Official**:
  - *Глубокоуважаемый(ая) [Имя Отчество]!* (Academic, institutional leadership).
  - *Уважаемый(ая) господин / госпожа [Фамилия]!* (Diplomatic, high external business).
  - *Уважаемый(ая) [Имя Отчество]!* (Standard professional).
- **Semi-Formal / Team**:
  - *Здравствуйте, [Имя]!*
  - *Добрый день, коллеги!*
- **Informal / Personal**:
  - *Привет, [Имя]!*
  - *Здравствуй, дорогой [Имя]!*

### 4.2 Valediction Formulas
- **Formal**:
  - *С уважением,* (With respect,)
  - *С наилучшими пожеланиями,* (With best wishes,)
  - *Искренне Ваш(а),* (Sincerely yours,)
- **Semi-Formal**:
  - *Хорошего дня!* (Have a nice day!)
  - *На связи,* (In touch,)
- **Informal**:
  - *До скорого!* / *До связи!* (See you soon!)
  - *Всего доброго!* / *Удачи!* (All the best! / Good luck!)
