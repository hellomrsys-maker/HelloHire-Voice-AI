# Vietnamese Morphological Analysis & Word Structure (Word Layer)

## 1. Monosyllabic Morpheme Architecture (Tiếng)
The fundamental morphological and phonological unit in Vietnamese is the **tiếng** (syllable-word). Every orthographic word token separated by spaces in standard Vietnamese text represents exactly one syllable:
- Over 80% of native morphemes are strictly monosyllabic.
- Complex words are formed by compounding (*từ ghép*) or phonological reduplication (*từ láy*).

## 2. Word Compounding Typology (Từ ghép)
1. **Coordinative Compounds (Từ ghép đẳng lập)**:
   - Formed by two morphemes of equal semantic status:
   - *bàn ghế* (table + chair = furniture)
   - *quần áo* (pants + shirt = clothes)
   - *nhà cửa* (house + door = home/property)
2. **Subordinative Compounds (Từ ghép chính phụ)**:
   - Formed by a head morpheme and a modifier:
   - *xe đạp* (vehicle + pedal = bicycle)
   - *máy bay* (machine + fly = airplane)
   - *hoa hồng* (flower + red/pink = rose)

## 3. Phonological Reduplication (Từ láy)
Reduplication is highly productive in Vietnamese, conveying emotional nuance, diminutives, intensity, or vivid imagery:
1. **Full Reduplication (Láy toàn bộ)**:
   - Identical repetition: *xanh xanh* (greenish), *trăng trắng* (whitish), *nhè nhẹ* (very gently).
2. **Partial Reduplication (Láy bộ phận)**:
   - **Alliteration / Initial Rhyme (Láy âm đầu)**: *đẹp đẽ* (beautiful), *sạch sẽ* (clean), *lung linh* (shimmering).
   - **Vowel / Final Rhyme (Láy vần)**: *lác đác* (scattered), *bối rối* (confused), *lúng túng* (awkward).

## 4. Preverbal Tense-Aspect-Mood (TAM) Particles
Temporal and aspectual status is conveyed entirely by free preverbal functional particles:

| Particle | Grammatical Function | Example | Meaning |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **đã** | Anterior / Past tense | *Tôi đã ăn cơm.* | I ate / have eaten. |
| **đang** | Continuous / Progressive aspect | *Anh đang làm việc.* | You/He is working. |
| **sẽ** | Posterior / Future tense | *Chúng tôi sẽ đi.* | We will go. |
| **vừa / mới** | Recent past / Immediate anterior | *Cô ấy vừa đến.* | She just arrived. |
| **chưa** | Experiential negative / Not yet | *Tôi chưa ăn.* | I have not yet eaten. |
| **không / chẳng** | Neutral declarative negation | *Anh ấy không biết.* | He does not know. |

## 5. Sino-Vietnamese Morphemes (Từ Hán-Việt)
Over 60% of Vietnamese formal, scientific, academic, and administrative vocabulary consists of Sino-Vietnamese morphemes borrowed historically from Classical Chinese:
- *quốc gia* (nation - 國家)
- *kinh tế* (economy - 經濟)
- *đại học* (university - 大學)
- *chính phủ* (government - 政府)
