# Vietnamese Semantic Representation & Idiomatic Space (Meaning Layer)

## 1. Numeral Classifiers (Loại từ / Danh từ chỉ loại)
In Vietnamese, nouns denoting discrete entities cannot be quantified directly by numerals without an intervening classifier:

| Classifier | Semantic Domain / Physical Geometry | Collocations |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **con** | Animals, living organisms, dynamic active objects | *con chó* (dog), *con mèo* (cat), *con dao* (knife), *con đường* (road) |
| **cái** | Inanimate artifacts, generic physical objects | *cái bàn* (table), *cái ghế* (chair), *cái áo* (shirt), *cái nhà* (house) |
| **quyển / cuốn** | Bound volumes, books, printed registers | *quyển sách* (book), *cuốn tiểu thuyết* (novel), *cuốn sổ* (notebook) |
| **cây** | Plants, trees, elongated cylindrical tools | *cây bút* (pen), *cây tre* (bamboo), *cây cầu* (bridge) |
| **bức** | Planar vertical sheets, wall hangings, messages | *bức tranh* (painting), *bức ảnh* (photograph), *bức thư* (letter), *bức tường* (wall) |
| **tờ** | Thin flat flexible sheets of paper/fabric | *tờ báo* (newspaper), *tờ giấy* (sheet of paper), *tờ tiền* (banknote) |
| **ngôi** | Elevated, sacred, or institutional buildings | *ngôi nhà* (home), *ngôi chùa* (pagoda), *ngôi trường* (school), *ngôi sao* (star) |
| **người** | Human beings (neutral/respectful) | *hai người bạn* (two friends), *người giáo viên* (the teacher) |
| **quả / trái** | Fruits, spherical or globular round items | *quả táo* (apple), *quả bóng* (ball), *trái tim* (heart) |

## 2. Classical Thành ngữ & Tục ngữ (Four-Syllable Idioms & Proverbs)
Vietnamese cultural expression is saturated with 4-syllable idiomatic formulas (*thành ngữ 4 chữ*):
- *Nước chảy đá mòn* (Water flows, stone wears away $\to$ Constant persistence achieves the impossible).
- *Uống nước nhớ nguồn* (When drinking water, remember the spring $\to$ Gratitude to one's origins and ancestors).
- *Ăn quả nhớ kẻ trồng cây* (When eating fruit, remember the one who planted the tree $\to$ Filial loyalty).
- *Đi một ngày đàng học một sàng khôn* (Travel one day on the road, learn a sieve full of wisdom $\to$ Travel broadens the mind).
- *Mưa dầm thấm lâu* (Persistent drizzle soaks deep $\to$ Gentle repetition leads to deep understanding).

## 3. Directional & Resultative Semantics
Verbs of motion function as secondary predicates expressing directional telicity:
- *ra* (outward, northwards): *đi ra* (go out)
- *vào* (inward, southwards): *đi vào* (go in)
- *lên* (upward): *đứng lên* (stand up)
- *xuống* (downward): *ngồi xuống* (sit down)
- *về* (returning to base/home): *về nhà* (return home)
- *lại* (towards speaker / again): *nói lại* (say again)
