# Vietnamese Phonology & Quốc Ngữ Orthography Specifications (Text/Sound Layer)

## 1. The Six Phonemic Lexical Tones (Sáu thanh điệu)
Standard Northern Vietnamese (Hanoi dialect) distinguishes six contrastive lexical pitch contours and phonation types:

| # | Tone Name | Diacritic Mark | Pitch Contour | Phonation / Contour Feature | Example | Meaning |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | **Ngang** (Level) | None (không dấu) | High Level (33 / 44) | Modal voice, sustained | *ma* | ghost |
| 2 | **Huyền** (Falling) | Dấu huyền ( \` ) | Low Falling (21) | Breathy voice / trailing | *mà* | but |
| 3 | **Sắc** (Rising) | Dấu sắc ( \' ) | Mid-High Rising (35) | Tense / sharp rise | *má* | mother / cheek |
| 4 | **Hỏi** (Dipping) | Dấu hỏi ( ̉ ) | Mid-Falling Rising (312) | Low dipping-rising | *mả* | tomb |
| 5 | **Ngã** (Glottalized) | Dấu ngã ( ~ ) | High Broken Rising (3ʔ5) | Glottalized interruption | *mã* | horse / code |
| 6 | **Nặng** (Drop) | Dấu nặng ( . ) | Low Dropping (21ʔ / 11) | Creaky voice, checked drop | *mạ* | rice seedling |

## 2. Syllable Canonical Structure (Cấu trúc âm tiết)
Every Vietnamese syllable conforms strictly to the maximal template:
$$\text{Syllable} = [C_1] + (w) + [V] + (C_2) + \text{Tone}$$
- $C_1$: Initial onset consonant (22 consonants in standard phonology: *b, c/k/q, ch, d/gi, đ, g/gh, h, kh, l, m, n, ng/ngh, nh, p, ph, r, s, t, th, tr, v, x*).
- $w$: Labio-velar glide medial (spelled *o* or *u*, e.g., *hoa, toán, qua*).
- $V$: Nuclear vowel (14 monophthongs and 3 closing diphthongs: *iê/yê/ia, ươ/ưa, uô/ua*).
- $C_2$: Coda consonant (*-m, -n, -ng, -nh* or unreleased stops *-p, -t, -c, -ch*).
- **Tone**: Spans the nuclear vowel and voiced coda.

## 3. Latin Quốc Ngữ Orthography & Diacritic Standard
Vietnamese is written in the Latin alphabet modified with 9 diacritics:
1. **Letter Alteration Diacritics**:
   - Breve: **ă** (short /a/)
   - Circumflex: **â** (short /ə/), **ê** (/e/), **ô** (/o/)
   - Horn: **ơ** (/əː/), **ư** (/ɯ/)
   - Bar: **đ** (voiced alveolar implosive /ɗ/, distinct from *d* which is /z/ in the North, /j/ in the South).
2. **Tone Diacritics**:
   - Placed directly on the principal nuclear vowel:
     - New standard (Quy tắc mới): placed on the second vowel in diphthongs ending in a coda (*hoán, hoàn*).
     - Traditional standard: placed on the first open vowel (*hoàn* vs *hòan*).
     - Tone diacritic stacks vertically above circumflex/horn (e.g. *thế, ở, được, một*).
