# Vietnamese Syntactic Structure & Grammar Specifications (Sentence Layer)

## 1. Canonical Word Order: Subject-Verb-Object (SVO)
Vietnamese belongs to the Vietic branch of the Austroasiatic language family. It is a strictly **analytic (isolating) SVO language**:
$$\text{Sentence} = [\text{Subject}] + [\text{TAM Preverbal Particle}] + [\text{Verb}] + [\text{Direct Object}] + [\text{Adverbial}]$$

### Syntactic Examples:
- *Tôi đọc sách.* (I read books.) — S - V - O
- *Anh ấy đang viết một bức thư.* (He is writing a letter.) — S - TAM - V - O
- *Mẹ mua ba con cá ở chợ.* (Mother bought three fish at the market.) — S - V - [Num + Clf + N] - PP

## 2. Head-Initial Noun Phrase Structure
Unlike Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, and Turkish which are strictly head-final in noun modification, Vietnamese is predominantly **head-initial**:
$$\text{Noun Phrase} = [\text{Quantifier / Number}] + [\text{Classifier (Loại từ)}] + [\text{Head Noun}] + [\text{Adjective / Relative Modifier}] + [\text{Demonstrative}]$$

### Examples:
- *ngôi nhà lớn này* = "this big house" (Classifier + Head Noun + Adjective + Demonstrative)
- *hai cuốn sách mới* = "two new books" (Number + Classifier + Head Noun + Adjective)
- *con mèo đen của tôi* = "my black cat" (Classifier + Noun + Adj + Possessive)

## 3. Topic-Comment Topology
Like many East and Southeast Asian languages, Vietnamese frequently employs topic-prominent constructions where the pragmatic theme or topic is fronted to clause-initial position:
- *Quyển sách này, tôi đã đọc rồi.* (This book, I already read [it].)
- *Hoa lan, tôi rất thích.* (Orchids, I like very much.)

## 4. Total Absence of Inflectional Morphology
Vietnamese exhibits zero morphological inflection:
- Zero verb conjugation for tense, person, or number.
- Zero grammatical gender.
- Zero noun case marking (relationships indicated entirely by word order and prepositions like *của* [of], *cho* [for], *với* [with], *ở* [at]).
- Zero plural inflection (plurality marked optionally by prenominal particles like *những* [definite plural], *các* [universal plural]).

## 5. Serial Verb Constructions (SVCs)
Multiple verbs can concatenate without conjunctions to convey sequential action, direction, or result:
- *Tôi đi mua thức ăn.* (I go buy food.)
- *Anh ấy chạy vào nhà.* (He ran enter house = He ran into the house.)
- *Chị ấy nói cho tôi nghe.* (She said for me listen = She told me.)
