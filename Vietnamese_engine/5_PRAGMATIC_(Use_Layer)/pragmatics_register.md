# Vietnamese Pragmatics, Register & Kinship Deixis (Use Layer)

## 1. Kinship Terms as Pronominal Deixis (Hệ thống đại từ nhân xưng thân tộc)
In Vietnamese, true grammatical pronouns (*tôi* [I], *chúng tôi* [we], *nó* [it/he/she-informal]) are rarely used in interpersonal interaction. Instead, social relationships are expressed through **hierarchical kinship terms** reflecting age, gender, and social status:

| Term | Literal Kinship | Used as "You" (Addressee) | Used as "I" (Self) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **anh** | Older brother | Male of slightly older or equal generation | Speaker addressing younger interlocutor |
| **chị** | Older sister | Female of slightly older or equal generation | Female speaker addressing younger interlocutor |
| **em** | Younger sibling | Interlocutor younger than speaker | Speaker addressing older interlocutor |
| **bác** | Parent's older sibling | Person older than one's parents | Elderly speaker addressing youths |
| **chú** | Father's younger brother | Male of father's younger generation | Mature male speaker to youths |
| **cô** | Father's sister / Female teacher | Female of father's generation / teacher | Female teacher / aunt to youths |
| **ông** | Grandfather | Elderly male / Highly respected gentleman | Grandfather to grandchildren |
| **bà** | Grandmother | Elderly female / Highly respected lady | Grandmother to grandchildren |
| **cháu** | Grandchild / Niece / Nephew | Child / Young interlocutor | Youth speaking to grandparents/elders |

### Conversational Concord Principle:
If the speaker addresses the listener as *anh* (older brother), the speaker must refer to themselves as *em* (younger sibling). Inverting or mixing kinship terms within a conversation is a severe pragmatic violation.

## 2. Politeness Final Particles (Trợ từ cuối câu)
- **ạ**: Obligatory sentence-final politeness marker when addressing superiors or elders:
  - *Dạ, vâng ạ.* (Yes, respectfully.)
  - *Em chào thầy ạ!* (I greet you, teacher [with deference]!)
- **nhé / nha**: Friendly, cordial suggestion or reassurance among peers:
  - *Đi nhé!* (Let's go, okay!)
- **dạ / vâng**: Deferential acknowledgments placed at turn-initial positions.

## 3. Epistolary & Administrative Standards (Thư tín hành chính)
Official Vietnamese correspondence mandates rigid ritual opening and closing formulas:
- **Salutation**:
  - High Diplomatic / Ministerial: *Kính gửi: Đồng chí Bộ trưởng ...*
  - Commercial / Corporate: *Kính gửi: Ban Giám đốc Công ty ...* / *Kính gửi Quý khách hàng,*
- **Closing**:
  - Formal / Official: *Trân trọng kính chào.* / *Kính chúc Quý công ty ngày càng phát triển.*
  - Corporate Standard: *Trân trọng,*
