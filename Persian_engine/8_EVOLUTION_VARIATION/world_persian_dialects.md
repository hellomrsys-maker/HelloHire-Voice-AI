# World Persian Dialects & Diachronic Evolution (Variation Layer)

## 1. The Greater Persophone Sphere
Persian is a pluricentric language with three official standard varieties across Western and Central Asia:
1. **Western Persian (Farsi - فارسی)**:
   - Official language of the Islamic Republic of Iran (~85M speakers).
   - Characterized by the loss of majhul vowels (/eː, oː/ merged into /iː, uː/), loss of post-vocalic /w/, and extensive colloquial vowel shifts.
2. **Eastern Persian (Dari - دری)**:
   - Official language of Afghanistan (~15M speakers).
   - Phonologically conservative; retains classical vowel distinctions (/eː/ *shēr* lion vs /iː/ *shīr* milk) and archaic consonant clusters.
3. **Northern Persian (Tajik - тоҷикӣ / فارسی تاجیکی)**:
   - Official language of Tajikistan and parts of Uzbekistan (~10M speakers).
   - Written in a modified Cyrillic script since 1940; vowel inventory altered under Russian and Uzbek influence (e.g. /ɒ/ $\to$ [ɔ]).

## 2. Diglossic Variation: Ketabi (Written) vs Tehrani (Spoken)
Modern Iranian Persian exhibits pronounced diglossia between the standard written literary standard (*zabān-e ketābi*) and colloquial Tehrani speech (*zabān-e mohāvere-i*):

1. **Vowel Shift: *ān* $\to$ *un***:
   - Before nasals, long /ɒː/ shifts to /uː/:
     - *tehrān* (تهران) $\to$ *tehrun*
     - *nān* (نان - bread) $\to$ *nun*
     - *khāneh* (خانه - house) $\to$ *khune*
     - *dāneshjūyān* (دانشجویان) $\to$ *dāneshjūyā*
2. **Verbal Contractions**:
   - 3rd person singular present: *mi-ravad* (می‌رود) $\to$ *mire*
   - Subjunctive: *be-ravad* (برود) $\to$ *bere*
   - Copula: *ast* (است) $\to$ *-e* (*ketāb ast* $\to$ *ketābe*)
3. **Plural Suffixes**:
   - Written *-hā* (ها) $\to$ spoken *-ā* (*ketābhā* $\to$ *ketābā*).

## 3. Lexical Borrowing Strata
- **Arabic Superstrate**: Over 40% of standard literary Persian vocabulary consists of Arabic loanwords, thoroughly adapted to Persian phonology and grammar.
- **French & Russian 19th/20th c. Technical Loans**: *mersi* (merci), *ānsānsor* (ascenseur), *samāvar* (samovar), *kālskeh* (kolyaska).
- **English Modern Tech Borrowings**: *kāmpuyuter*, *lap-top*, *fāyl*, *ap* (app).
