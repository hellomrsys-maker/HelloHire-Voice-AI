# Persian Morphological Analysis & Word Structure (Word Layer)

## 1. Dual-Stem Verbal System
Every Persian verb has two distinct lexical stems:
1. **Past Stem (بن ماضی)**: Formed regularly by dropping the infinitive suffix *-an* or *-tan*:
   - *raftan* (to go) $\to$ *raft-*
   - *khordan* (to eat) $\to$ *khord-*
   - *kardan* (to do) $\to$ *kard-*
   - *didan* (to see) $\to$ *did-*
2. **Present Stem (بن مضارع)**: Historically derived from Old Persian present stems; semi-regular or irregular:
   - *raftan* $\to$ *rav-*
   - *khordan* $\to$ *khor-*
   - *kardan* $\to$ *kon-*
   - *didan* $\to$ *bin-*
   - *goftan* (to say) $\to$ *gu-*
   - *dāshtan* (to have) $\to$ *dār-*

## 2. Personal Endings (شناسه‌های فعلی)
Finite verbs append 6 personal agreement suffixes:

| Person | Pronoun | Present Ending | Past Ending |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1st Sg | *man* (من) | *-am* (-َم) | *-am* (-َم) |
| 2nd Sg | *to* (تو) | *-i* (-ی) | *-i* (-ی) |
| 3rd Sg | *u* (او) | *-ad* (-َد) | $\varnothing$ (zero) |
| 1st Pl | *mā* (ما) | *-im* (-یم) | *-im* (-یم) |
| 2nd Pl | *shomā* (شما) | *-id* (-ید) | *-id* (-ید) |
| 3rd Pl | *ānhā* (آن‌ها) | *-and* (-َند) | *-and* (-َند) |

## 3. Verbal Aspect & Mood Prefixes
1. **Durative / Imperfective Prefix (*mi-* / میـ)**:
   - Attached with a Zero-Width Non-Joiner (ZWNJ / `\u200c`):
   - Present Continuous: *mi-khor-am* (می‌خورم) = "I eat / I am eating"
   - Past Continuous: *mi-khord-am* (می‌خوردم) = "I was eating / I used to eat"
2. **Subjunctive / Imperative Prefix (*be-* / بـ)**:
   - *be-khor-am* (بخورم) = "(that) I eat"
   - *be-khor!* (بخور!) = "Eat!"
3. **Negative Prefix (*na-* / نـ, *ne-* before *mi-*)**:
   - *na-khor-am* (نخورم) = "I don't eat (subjunctive)"
   - *ne-mi-khor-am* (نمی‌خورم) = "I do not eat"

## 4. Nominal Morphology & Pluralization
- **Regular Suffix *-hā* (ها)**: Universal, animates and inanimates:
  - *ketāb* $\to$ *ketāb-hā* (کتاب‌ها)
  - *mard* $\to$ *mard-hā* (مردها)
- **Animate Suffix *-ān* (ان)**: Used primarily for animate nouns and formal register:
  - *mard* $\to$ *mard-ān* (مردان)
  - *derakht* $\to$ *derakht-ān* (درختان)
- **Arabic Loan Plurals**:
  - Broken plurals (*jam'-e mokassar*): *ketāb* $\to$ *kotob*, *nazar* $\to$ *ānrā'*, *taraf* $\to$ *atrāf*.
  - Feminine plural *-āt* (-ات): *ettelā'āt* (اطلاعات).

## 5. Pronominal Enclitics (ضمایر متصل)
Enclitics express possessive relationship on nouns or object agreement on verbs:
- *-am* (م-) = my / me
- *-at* (ت-) = your / you (sg)
- *-ash* (ش-) = his/her / him/her
- *-emān* (مان-) = our / us
- *-etān* (تان-) = your / you (pl)
- *-eshān* (شان-) = their / them
- Example: *ketāb-am* (کتابم) = "my book".
