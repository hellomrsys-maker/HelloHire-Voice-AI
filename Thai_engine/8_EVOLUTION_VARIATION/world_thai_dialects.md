### 2. Major Geographic Dialects

1. **Central Thai (ภาษากลาง / ภาษาไทยถิ่นกลาง)**:
   - Official national standard spoken in Bangkok and the Chao Phraya river basin. Standard 5-tone system, baseline for education, government, and media.
2. **Isan / Northeastern Thai (ภาษาอีสาน)**:
   - Spoken by 20M+ people in Northeast Thailand; closely related to Lao. Features 6 tones, distinct lexical items (*แซ่บ* *sɛ́ɛp* - delicious, *เฮ็ด* *hèt* - do, *บ่* *bɔ̀ɔ* - not).
3. **Northern Thai / Kam Mueang (ภาษาเหนือ / คำเมือง)**:
   - Historical language of the Lan Na Kingdom. Preserves 6 tones and distinctive intonation (*เจ้า* *câaw* female polite particle).
4. **Southern Thai / Pak Tai (ภาษาใต้)**:
   - Characterized by rapid speech cadence, severe syllable clipping, and up to 7 distinct tone contours (*หรอย* *rɔ̌ɔy* - delicious).
