### 2. Traditional Thai Proverbs (*สุภาษิต*) & Cultural Idioms

| Proverb (Thai Script) | Transliteration | Literal Meaning | Pragmatic Equivalent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **น้ำขึ้นให้รีบตัก** | *nám khɯ̂n hây rîip tàk* | "When the tide rises, hurry to scoop the water." | Strike while the iron is hot; seize opportune moments. |
| **ช้างตายทั้งตัวเอาใบบัวมาปิด** | *cháaŋ taay tháŋ tua aw bay bua maa pìt* | "A whole dead elephant cannot be hidden with a single lotus leaf." | A massive scandal or grave deed cannot be concealed by petty lies. |
| **เข้าเมืองตาหลิ่ว ต้องหลิ่วตาตาม** | *khâw mɯaŋ taa lìw, tɔ̂ŋ lìw taa taam* | "Entering a city of winking people, one must wink along." | When in Rome, do as the Romans do; adapt to local customs. |
| **ไก่งามเพราะขน คนงามเพราะแต่ง** | *kày ŋaam phrɔ́ khǒn, khon ŋaam phrɔ́ tɛ̀ŋ* | "A fowl is fair by its feathers; a human is fair by attire." | Fine feathers make fine birds; grooming and conduct elevate human dignity. |
| **ใจเย็น ๆ** | *cay yen yen* | "Cool heart" | Calm down; keep your composure; be patient. |
