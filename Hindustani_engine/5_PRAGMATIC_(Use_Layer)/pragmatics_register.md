# Hindustani Pragmatics, Sociolinguistics & Register (Use Layer)

## 1. Honorific System & Social Deixis
Hindustani interpersonal pragmatics revolves around hierarchical social deference, kinship distance, and mutual respect (*adab* / *lihaz*).

### The Three Address Levels:
1. **Tier 3: आप (Aap) - Deferential / Formal**:
   - Compulsory for elders, professors, clients, strangers, officials, and public figures.
   - Demands 3rd person plural honorific verb agreement: *Aap kya karte hain?*
   - Often reinforced with the suffix **जी (ji)**: *Doctor saab ji, Mata ji, Pandit ji*.
2. **Tier 2: तुम (Tum) - Familiar / Equal**:
   - Used among close friends, classmates of equal standing, siblings, spouses (in modern urban contexts).
   - Verb agreement: *Tum kya karte ho?*
3. **Tier 1: तू (Tu) - Intimate / Non-Formal**:
   - Deep intimacy (mothers to small children, ancient poets to God), or extreme informal solidarity between lifelong best friends.
   - Severely insulting and offensive if used towards strangers or elders.
   - Verb agreement: *Tu kya karta hai?*

## 2. Register Continuum: Shuddh Hindi vs. Saaf Urdu vs. Bazaar Hindustani
- **Shuddh Hindi (High Sanskritized Register)**:
  - Formal government, state broadcasting, academic literature in India.
  - Characterized by Sanskrit Tatsama loanwords: *dhanyavaad, vishwavidalaya, prabandhan, samasya*.
- **Khaalis Urdu (High Perso-Arabic Register)**:
  - High literature, poetry (*ghazal*), formal discourse in Pakistan.
  - Characterized by Persian/Arabic loans: *shukriya, tehzeeb, intizam, mushkil, aalam*.
- **Bazaar / Bolchaal Hindustani (Standard Spoken)**:
  - Spoken lingua franca of Bollywood, commerce, everyday life across the subcontinent.
  - Balanced synthesis of both vocabularies.

## 3. Epistolary & Greeting Formulas
- **Greetings**:
  - General / Secular: *Namaste, Namaskar, Adaab, Adaab Arz Hai, Salaam*.
- **Formal Closings**:
  - *Aapka kripa-kaankshi* (आपका कृपाकांक्षी) / *Bhavadiya* (भवदीय) - Formal Hindi.
  - *Niyazmand* (نیازمند) / *Mukhlis* (مخلص) - Formal Urdu.
  - *Aapka / Aapki* (आपका / आपकी) - Universal standard.
