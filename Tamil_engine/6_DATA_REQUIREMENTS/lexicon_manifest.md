# Data Requirements & Lexicon Manifest for Tamil (Corpus Layer)
## Sovereign Engine: `Tamil_engine` | Dravidian Lexicon

### 1. Primary Lexicons & Benchmark Corpora

1. **Madras University Tamil Lexicon (MTL)**:
   - Scope: 6 volumes + supplementary volume, >124,405 headwords.
   - Coverage: Sangam classical archaic vocabulary through 20th-century scientific coinages.
2. **Universal Dependencies (UD) Tamil (TTB - Tamil Treebank)**:
   - Syntactic dependency gold standard covering SOV clause structures, case postpositions, and non-finite verbal chains.
3. **Tamil Virtual Academy (TVA) Corpus & Dictionary**:
   - Official government lexicon standardizing modern administrative, legal, and educational Tamil terminology.
4. **Sangam Literature Text Bank**:
   - *Pattuppāṭṭu* (Ten Idylls) and *Eṭṭuttokai* (Eight Anthologies) for classical archaic syntax and morphological derivation.

### 2. Unicode Specifications & Script Mapping
- **Tamil Unicode Block**: `U+0B80` – `U+0BFF`
  - Vowels: `U+0B85` – `U+0B94`
  - Consonants: `U+0B95` – `U+0BB9`
  - Pulli (Virama): `U+0BCD` (்)
  - Vowel Signs: `U+0BBE` – `U+0BCC`
  - Aytham: `U+0B83` (ஃ)
- **Romanization**: ISO 15919 standard transliteration for precise phonemic distinction (e.g. *t, ṭ, ṟ, n, ṇ, ṉ, l, ḷ, ḻ*).
