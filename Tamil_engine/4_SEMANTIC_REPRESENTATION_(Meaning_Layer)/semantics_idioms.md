# Semantic Representation & Idiomatic Lore for Tamil (Meaning Layer)
## Sovereign Engine: `Tamil_engine` | Sangam & Classical Semantics

### 1. Conceptual Frameworks: Sangam *Tiṇai* & The *Tirukkuṟaḷ*
Tamil semantics is anchored in over 2,500 years of unbroken literary heritage:

1. **The Classical *Tiṇai* (திணை) Eco-Semantic Matrix**:
   - **Kuriñci** (குறிஞ்சி - Mountainous): Union of lovers, fragrance of venkai flowers.
   - **Mullai** (முல்லை - Pastoral / Forest): Patient waiting, home-coming, evening rain.
   - **Marutam** (மருதம் - Agricultural / Riverine): Domestic life, lovers' quarrels, fertile riverbanks.
   - **Neytal** (நெய்தல் - Littoral / Seashore): Yearning, sorrow of separation, salt marshes.
   - **Pālai** (பாலை - Arid / Wasteland): Separation, dangerous journeys through desolate tracts.

2. **The Four Sovereign Aims (*Puruṣārtha* / உறுதிப்பொருள்)**:
   - **அறம்** (*Aṟam* - Righteousness / Virtue)
   - **பொருள்** (*Poruḷ* - Wealth / Material prosperity)
   - **இன்பம்** (*Iṉpam* - Love / Aesthetic joy)
   - **வீடு** (*Vīṭu* - Spiritual liberation)

### 3. Dravidian Kinship Semantic Bifurcation
Tamil kinship terminology rigorously encodes relative age (elder vs younger) and parallel vs cross relations:

- **Elder / Younger Distinction**:
  - *அண்ணன்* (*aṇṇaṉ* - elder brother) vs *தம்பி* (*tampi* - younger brother).
  - *அக்கா* (*akkā* - elder sister) vs *தங்கை* (*taṅkai* - younger sister).
- **Cross-Cousin / Affinal Relations**:
  - *மாமா* (*māmā* - maternal uncle or father-in-law).
  - *அத்தை* (*attai* - paternal aunt or mother-in-law).
  - *மச்சான்* (*maccāṉ* - brother-in-law or cross-cousin).
