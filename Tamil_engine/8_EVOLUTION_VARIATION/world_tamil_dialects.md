### 2. Major World & Regional Dialects

1. **Central Tamil Nadu (Thanjavur - Tiruchirappalli)**:
   - Considered the normative phonological baseline for standard spoken Tamil (*Koṭuntamiḻ*).
2. **Sri Lankan / Jaffna (*Yāḻppāṇam*) Tamil**:
   - Extremely conservative phonology; preserves Sangam and medieval distinctions lost in mainland India (e.g. distinct pronunciation of dental plosives and retention of Old Tamil demonstratives *untu*).
3. **Singapore & Malaysian Tamil**:
   - Official national language of Singapore; strictly standardized around the modern literary curriculum with localized administrative loan adaptions.
4. **Kongu Tamil (Western Tamil Nadu)**:
   - Characterized by unique polite second-person suffixes (*-ṅga* / *-unka*) and retention of the alveolar lateral approximant.
5. **Chennai / Northern Tamil (Madras Bashai)**:
   - High-contact urban contact dialect blending Tamil grammatical frames with Telugu, Urdu, and English lexical roots.
