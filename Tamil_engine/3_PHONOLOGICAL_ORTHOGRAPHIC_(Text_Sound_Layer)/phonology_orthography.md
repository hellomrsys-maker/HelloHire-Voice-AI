# Phonological & Orthographic Specification for Tamil (Sound & Text Layer)
## Sovereign Engine: `Tamil_engine` | Dravidian Phonology

### 1. Tamil Script System (247 Characters)
Tamil is written in an **abugida** where each base consonant carries an inherent neutral short vowel /a/ unless canceled by a virama/pulli (*புள்ளி* dot above) or combined with a vowel diacritic:

$$\text{Total Alphabet} = 12 \text{ (Vowels)} + 18 \text{ (Consonants)} + 216 \text{ (Consonant-Vowel Compounds)} + 1 \text{ (Āytam)} = 247 \text{ Glyphs}$$

### 3. The 18 Consonants (*Meyyeḻuttu* மெய்யெழுத்து)
Categorized into three traditional classical classes based on articulatory pressure:

#### A. Hard Consonants (*Valliṉam* வல்லினம் - Plosives)
1. **க்** (*k*): Velar plosive /k/ (voiced /ɡ/ or /x/ intervocalically)
2. **ச்** (*c*): Palatal affricate /t͡ʃ/ (fricative /s/ intervocalically)
3. **ட்** (*ṭ*): Retroflex plosive /ʈ/ (voiced /ɖ/ intervocalically)
4. **த்** (*t*): Dental plosive /t̪/ (voiced /d̪/ intervocalically)
5. **ப்** (*p*): Bilabial plosive /p/ (voiced /b/ or /β/ intervocalically)
6. **ற்** (*ṟ*): Alveolar plosive / trill /r/ or /tr/

#### B. Soft Consonants (*Melliṉam* மெல்லினம் - Nasals)
1. **ங்** (*ṅ*): Velar nasal /ŋ/
2. **ஞ்** (*ñ*): Palatal nasal /ɲ/
3. **ண்** (*ṇ*): Retroflex nasal /ɳ/
4. **ந்** (*n*): Dental nasal /n̪/
5. **ம்** (*m*): Bilabial nasal /m/
6. **ன்** (*ṉ*): Alveolar nasal /n/

#### C. Medial Consonants (*Iṭaiyiṉam* இடையினம் - Approximants & Liquids)
1. **ய்** (*y*): Palatal approximant /j/
2. **ர்** (*r*): Alveolar flap /ɾ/
3. **ல்** (*l*): Alveolar lateral approximant /l/
4. **வ்** (*v*): Labiodental approximant /ʋ/
5. **ழ்** (*ḻ*): **Voiced Retroflex Central Approximant** /ɻ/ (the hallmark of Tamil, as in *தமிழ்* *Tamiḻ*)
6. **ள்** (*ḷ*): Retroflex lateral approximant /ɭ/

#### D. The Solitary Aytham (*Āytaveḻuttu* ஆய்தவெழுத்து)
- **ஃ** (*k* / *akh*): Voiceless glottal fricative /h/ or gemination inducer, e.g. *அஃது* (*aḵtu*).

### 4. Tri-Way Coronal & Retroflex Phonology
A signature phonetic feature of Dravidian linguistics:

```
          Dental      Alveolar    Retroflex
Stops:      த் (t)       ற் (ṟ)       ட் (ṭ)
Nasals:     ந் (n)       ன் (ṉ)       ண் (ṇ)
Liquids:    —           ல் (l)       ள் (ḷ) / ழ் (ḻ)
```

- Confusion of *ṉ* (ன்) and *ṇ* (ண்), or *l* (ல்), *ḷ* (ள்), and *ḻ* (ழ்) constitutes an orthographic and phonemic error:
  - *கல்* (*kal* - stone) vs *கள்* (*kaḷ* - toddy).
  - *வாலி* (*vāli* - bucket) vs *வாளி* (*vāḷi* - pail) vs *வாழி* (*vāḻi* - may you live!).
