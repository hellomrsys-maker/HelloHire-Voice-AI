# Layer 4: Semantic Representation (Meaning Layer) - Bengali Engine (বাংলা)

## 1. Compound Verbs & Vector Verbs (*যৌগিক ক্রিয়া* - Yougik Kriya)
Bengali serial verb constructions pair a non-finite conjunctive participle (stem + *-e*) with an auxiliary/vector verb that strips its literal lexical meaning to express aspectual Aktionsart, telicity, affectedness, or emotional nuance:

| Vector Verb | Literal Meaning | Semantic Contribution in Compound | Exemplar | Idiomatic Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ফেলা** (`phela`) | to drop / cast | Irrevocable completion, finality, rashness | *করে ফেলা* (`kore phela`) | "To finish doing completely" |
| **রাখা** (`rakha`) | to keep / put | Preparatory action, sustained preservation | *লিখে রাখা* (`likhe rakha`) | "To note down for the future" |
| **দেওয়া** (`dewa`) | to give | Action performed for the benefit of another | *বলে দেওয়া* (`bole dewa`) | "To explain / tell for someone" |
| **নেওয়া** (`newa`) | to take | Action performed for self-benefit or inward | *পড়ে নেওয়া* (`pore newa`) | "To read through for oneself" |
| **ওঠা** (`othha`) | to rise / climb | Sudden inception, emergence, surprise | *হেসে ওঠা* (`heshe othha`) | "To burst into laughter" |
| **বসা** (`bosa`) | to sit | Inadvertent, reckless, or inappropriate act | *করে বসা* (`kore bosa`) | "To do something foolishly" |
| **যাওয়া** (`jawa`) | to go | Gradual completion, transition of state | *শুকিয়ে যাওয়া* (`shukiye jawa`) | "To dry up completely" |
| **আসা** (`asha`) | to come | Progression toward the present moment | *চলে আসা* (`chole asha`) | "To arrive / come away" |

---

## 2. Conjunct Verbs (*যুক্ত ক্রিয়া* - Jukto Kriya)
Bengali forms extensive verbal concepts by compounding a nominal or adjectival base with a light support verb (*করা* `kora` - do, *হওয়া* `howa` - be/become, *পাওয়া* `pawa` - get, *দেওয়া* `dewa` - give):
- *কাজ করা* (`kaj kora` - work do) = "to work"
- *শুরু করা* (`shuru kora` - beginning do) = "to start (transitive)"
- *শুরু হওয়া* (`shuru howa` - beginning become) = "to start (intransitive)"
- *বিশ্বাস করা* (`bishwash kora` - faith do) = "to believe / trust"
- *মনে রাখা* (`mone rakha` - mind-in keep) = "to remember"
- *মনে পড়া* (`mone poda` - mind-in fall) = "to recall / come to mind"

## 3. Dative / Experiencer Subject Constructions
In sensory, psychological, and physiological states, the semantic experiencer is marked with the **Genitive case** (*আমার* `amar`, *তার* `tar`, *বাবার* `babar`) or Objective case (*আমাকে* `amake`), while the syntactic verb remains 3rd person singular neuter:
- *আমার খুব ভালো লাগছে।* (`Amar khub bhalo lagchhe`) - "I am feeling very good" (lit. "Of me very good is feeling").
- *তার ঠান্ডা লাগছে।* (`Tar thanda lagchhe`) - "He is feeling cold."
- *আমার খিদে পেয়েছে।* (`Amar khide peyechhe`) - "I am hungry" (lit. "Of me hunger has caught").
- *তোমাকে যেতে হবে।* (`Tomake jete hobe`) - "You have to go" (lit. "To you to go will be").

## 4. High-Frequency Bengali Idioms & Metaphorical Expressions
- *মাথা খারাপ করা* (`matha kharap kora` - head bad do) = "To drive crazy / lose one's mind"
- *আকাশ থেকে পড়া* (`akash theke poda` - fall from the sky) = "To be utterly astounded / taken aback"
- *চোখে ধুলো দেওয়া* (`chokhe dhulo dewa` - dust in eyes give) = "To deceive / pull wool over someone's eyes"
- *গা বাঁচিয়ে চলা* (`ga bãchiye chola` - body saving walk) = "To steer clear of trouble / avoid responsibility"
- *ঘোড়ার ডিম* (`ghodar dim` - horse's egg) = "Nonsense / something nonexistent"
- *লেজেগোবরে করা* (`lejegobore kora`) = "To botch or make a complete mess of things"
