# Layer 5: Pragmatic Layer & Register Stratification - Bengali Engine (বাংলা)

## 1. Diglossia: Sadhu Bhasha (সাধু ভাষা) vs. Cholito Bhasha (চলিত ভাষা)
Standard Bengali is characterized by an acute literary diglossia between two registers:

| Feature | Sadhu Bhasha (সাধু ভাষা) - Formal / Classical | Cholito Bhasha (চলিত ভাষা) - Modern Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Historical Role** | 19th-early 20th century prose, legal, religious, formal scripture | Contemporary spoken, modern journalism, literature, daily media |
| **Vocabulary Source** | Heavily *Tatsama* (direct Sanskrit loanwords) | *Tadbhav* (Prakrit derivatives), *Deshi* (indigenous), loanwords |
| **Verbal Inflection** | Full uncontracted suffixes: *করিতেছেন* (`koritechhen`), *হইয়াছিল* (`hoiyachhilo`) | Contracted modern suffixes: *করছেন* (`korchhen`), *হয়েছিল* (`hoyechhilo`) |
| **Pronouns** | Full archaic forms: *যাঁহার* (`jãhar`), *তাহারা* (`tahara`), *উহার* (`uhar`) | Modern forms: *যার* (`jar`), *তারা* (`tara`), *ওর* (`or`) |
| **Rule Invariant** | **Guru-Chondali Dosh (গুরুচণ্ডালী দোষ)**: Mixing Sadhu and Cholito forms in the same clause is an egregious stylistic solecism. |

## 2. Three-Tier Address Hierarchy & Pragmatic Politeness
Bengali strictly enforces a 3-tier social distance and politeness matrix across pronouns and verbal agreement:

```
[Social Hierarchy Tiers]:
1. Honorific / Distant (আপনি - Aapni) -> Verb ending: -en / -un
2. Familiar / Equal (তুমি - Tumi)    -> Verb ending: -o
3. Intimate / Inferior (তুই - Tui)    -> Verb ending: -ish / -i
```

### Pronoun & Verbal Agreement Paradigm:
- **Honorific (আপনি / তিনি / আপনারা / তাঁরা)**:
  - Addressed to elders, teachers, superiors, strangers, and respected figures.
  - Suffixes: *বলেন* (`bolen` - present), *বলুন* (`bolun` - imperative), *বললেন* (`bollen` - past), *বলবেন* (`bolben` - future).
  - 3rd person honorific pronoun is *তিনি* (`tini`), possessive *তাঁর* (`tãr`).
- **Familiar (তুমি / তোমরা)**:
  - Addressed to friends, peers, close younger relatives, spouse, and colleagues.
  - Suffixes: *বলো* (`bolo` - present/imperative), *বললে* (`bolle` - past), *বলবে* (`bolbe` - future).
- **Intimate / Subordinate (তুই / তোরা)**:
  - Addressed to very intimate childhood friends, very young children, pets, or in moments of extreme anger/disdain.
  - Suffixes: *বলিস* (`bolish` - present/future), *বল* (`bol` - imperative), *বললি* (`bolli` - past).

## 3. Honorific Particles & Courtesy Affixes
- **মশাই / মহাশয়** (`moshai / mohashoy`): Polite title affixed to names or professions (*ডাক্তারবাবু / ডাক্তার মশাই* `daktarmoshai`).
- **বাবু** (`babu`): Traditional respectful gentleman title (*সুমনবাবু* `Suman-babu`).
- **আজ্ঞে / হ্যাঁ** (`aagye / hã`): "Yes, sir/ma'am" respectful acknowledgment.
- **দয়া করে / অনুগ্রহ করে** (`doya kore / onugroho kore`): "Please / kindly" markers.

## 4. Epistolary Formulas & Register Templates

### Formal Business / Academic Correspondence:
- **Salutation**: *শ্রদ্ধেয় মহাশয় / মহাশয়া* (`Shroddheyo mohashoy / mohashoya` - Respected Sir/Madam).
- **Opening**: *বিনীত নিবেদন এই যে...* (`Binito nibedon ei je...` - Humble submission is that...).
- **Valediction**: *আপনার বিশ্বস্ত / ভবদীয়* (`Aapnar bishwashyo / Bhobodeeyo` - Yours faithfully / sincerely).

### Familiar / Informal Letters:
- **Salutation**: *প্রিয় [নাম]* (`Priyo [naam]` - Dear [name]).
- **Opening**: *আশা করি ভালো আছ।* (`Asha kori bhalo aachho` - Hope you are well).
- **Valediction**: *ইতি, তোমার স্নেহের / ইতি, তোমার বন্ধু* (`Iti, tomar sneher / Iti, tomar bondhu` - Sincerely, yours affectionately / your friend).
