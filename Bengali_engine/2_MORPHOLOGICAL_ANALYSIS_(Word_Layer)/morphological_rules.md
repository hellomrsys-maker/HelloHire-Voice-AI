# Layer 2: Morphological Analysis (Word Layer) - Bengali Engine (বাংলা)

## 1. Nominal Classifiers & Definiteness Markers (*নির্দেশক* - Nirdeshok)
Bengali lacks definite and indefinite articles (*the*, *a*). Instead, definiteness, countability, and noun category are mediated through bound classifier enclitics attached directly to the noun or preceding numeral:

| Classifier | Semantic Domain | Exemplar | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **-টা** (`-Ta`) | General singular / inanimate / default definite | *বইটা* (`boi-Ta`) | "The book" |
| **-টি** (`-Ti`) | Diminutive / affectionate / polite singular | *কলমটি* (`kolom-Ti`) | "The pen (polite/small)" |
| **-গুলো** (`-gulo`) | General plural definite | *বইগুলো* (`boi-gulo`) | "The books" |
| **-গুলি** (`-guli`) | Formal / polite plural definite | *কথাগুলি* (`kotha-guli`) | "The words / matters" |
| **-খানা** (`-khana`) | Flat, broad, rectangular objects (books, letters) | *চিঠিখানা* (`chithi-khana`) | "The letter" |
| **-খানি** (`-khani`) | Diminutive / poetic flat object | *মুখখানি* (`mukh-khani`) | "The sweet face" |
| **-জন** (`-jon`) | Human beings (preceded by numeral) | *পাঁচজন ছাত্র* (`pãch-jon chatro`) | "Five students" |

## 2. Four Verbal Root Conjugation Classes
Bengali verbs are categorized into four structural stem classes governed by the root vowel:

1. **Class 1 (Regular -a stem)**: e.g., *করা* (`kora` - to do), *পড়া* (`poda` - to read), *বলা* (`bola` - to say).
   - High stem vowel shift in 1st person & non-finite forms: root vowel /ɔ/ shifts to /o/ (*kori*, *kore*).
2. **Class 2 (Vowel stem -e/-i)**: e.g., *দেওয়া* (`dewa` - to give), *নেওয়া* (`newa` - to take), *খাওয়া* (`khawa` - to eat).
   - Stem changes across aspect: *dei* (I give), *dilo* (he gave), *diyechi* (I have given).
3. **Class 3 (High vowel -i stem)**: e.g., *কেনা* (`kena` - to buy), *চেনা* (`chena` - to recognize).
   - Umlaut shift: *kini* (I buy), *kinlam* (I bought), *kene* (he buys).
4. **Class 4 (Round vowel -u/-o stem)**: e.g., *শোওয়া* (`showa` - to sleep/lie down), *ধোওয়া* (`dhowa` - to wash).
   - Umlaut shift: *shui* (I sleep), *shuyechi* (I have slept), *sholo* (he slept).

## 3. Vowel Harmony & Stem Apophony (*অভিশ্রুতি* - Ovisruti)
Bengali displays pervasive regressive vowel harmony where high vowels (/i/, /u/) in a suffix trigger raising of the preceding root vowel:
- Low-mid back /ɔ/ $\to$ High-mid back /o/: *করা* (`kɔra`) $\to$ *করি* (`kori`).
- Low front /æ/ $\to$ High-mid front /e/: *দেখা* (`dækha`) $\to$ *দেখি* (`dekhi`).
- Low central /a/ $\to$ High-mid front /e/ (in dialectal/contracted forms): *করিয়া* (`koriya`) $\to$ *করে* (`kore`).

## 4. Finite Verbal Paradigm: Person & Honorific Concord
Bengali verbs inflect for person (1st, 2nd, 3rd) and politeness hierarchy, but **never for gender or number**:

| Tense / Aspect | 1st Person (আমি/আমরা) | 2nd Ordinary (তুমি) | 2nd Intimate (তুই) | 2nd/3rd Honorific (আপনি/তিনি) | 3rd Ordinary (সে/তারা) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Simple Present** | করি (`kori`) | করো (`koro`) | করিস (`korish`) | করেন (`koren`) | করে (`kore`) |
| **Present Continuous** | করছি (`korchhi`) | করছ (`korchho`) | করছিস (`korchhish`) | করছেন (`korchhen`) | করছে (`korchhe`) |
| **Present Perfect** | করেছি (`korechhi`) | করেছ (`korechho`) | করেছিস (`korechhish`) | করেছেন (`korechhen`) | করেছে (`korechhe`) |
| **Simple Past** | করলাম (`korlam`) | করলে (`korle`) | করলি (`korli`) | করলেন (`korlen`) | করল (`korlo`) |
| **Past Continuous** | করছিলাম (`korchhilam`) | করছিলে (`korchhile`) | করছিলি (`korchhiti`) | করছিলেন (`korchholen`) | করছিল (`korchholo`) |
| **Past Perfect** | করেছিলাম (`korechhilam`) | করেছিলে (`korechhile`) | করেছিলি (`korechhili`) | করেছিলেন (`korechholen`) | করেছিল (`korechholo`) |
| **Habitual Past** | করতাম (`kortam`) | করতে (`korte`) | করতিস (`kortish`) | করতেন (`korten`) | করত (`korto`) |
| **Simple Future** | করব (`korbo`) | করবে (`korbe`) | করবি (`korbi`) | করবেন (`korben`) | করবে (`korbe`) |
| **Imperative** | — | করো (`koro`) | কর (`kor`) | করুন (`korun`) | — |

## 5. Non-Finite Morphology
- **Conjunctive (`-e`)**: Stem + *-e* (*করে* `kore`, *দেখে* `dekhe`, *গিয়ে* `giye`).
- **Purposive / Infinitive (`-te`)**: Stem + *-te* (*করতে* `korte`, *যেতে* `jete`, *বলতে* `bolte`).
- **Conditional (`-le`)**: Stem + *-le* (*করলে* `korle`, *গেলে* `gele`, *দেখলে* `dekhle`).
- **Verbal Noun (`-a` / `-on`)**: *পড়া* (`poda` - reading), *গমন* (`gomon` - going).
