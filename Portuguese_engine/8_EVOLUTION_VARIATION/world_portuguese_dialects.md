Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR)                                      European / African Norms (pt-PT / PALOP)

---

## 2. Regional Varieties in Brazil
- **Paulistano / Caipira (São Paulo & Interior)**: Syllable-timed rhythm, retroflex or alveolar tap rhotics, strict preservation of [s] coda.
- **Carioca (Rio de Janeiro)**: Postalveolar sibilant coda ([ʃ], [ʒ]), uvular trill [ʁ].
- **Nordestino (Northeastern Brazil)**: Distinctive vowel opening, widespread retention of *tu* with 3rd-person verbs (*tu vai*).
- **Gaúcho (Southern Brazil)**: Use of *tu* with 2nd-person verb inflections (*tu falas*), influenced by neighboring Spanish.

---

## 3. African Varieties (PALOP)
- **Angolan Portuguese (pt-AO)**: Close to pt-PT in phonology and enclisis, with Kimbundu and Umbundu substrate lexical borrowings (*bwe, kota, cota*).
- **Mozambican Portuguese (pt-MZ)**: Influenced by Bantu languages (Makhuwa, Tsonga); distinctive prepositional omission.
