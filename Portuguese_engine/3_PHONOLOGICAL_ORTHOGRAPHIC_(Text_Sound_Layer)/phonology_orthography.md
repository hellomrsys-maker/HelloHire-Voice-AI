# Layer 3: Phonological & Orthographic Layer - Portuguese Engine (Português)

## 1. Orthographic System & Diacritical Markers
Portuguese uses the Latin script augmented by specific diacritics:
- **Acute Accent (´)**: Marks tonic open vowels (*á, é, í, ó, ú*): *café, avó, está*.
- **Circumflex Accent (^)**: Marks tonic closed/nasal vowels (*â, ê, ô*): *você, avô, lâmpada*.
- **Tilde (~)**: Marks phonemic nasal vowels and diphthongs (*ã, õ*): *mão, pão, corações, alemães*.
- **Grave Accent (`)**: Used exclusively to indicate crase contraction (*à, às, àquele*).
- **Cedilla (ç)**: Marks voiceless dental/alveolar sibilant [s] before *a, o, u*: *cabeça, coração, açúcar*.
- **Hyphen (-)**: Links enclitic and mesoclitic pronouns (*disse-me, dir-te-ei*) and compound nouns (*guarda-chuva*).

## 2. Acordo Ortográfico de 1990 (AO90) Invariants
The 1990 Orthographic Agreement unified spelling between Brazil, Portugal, and the PALOP nations:
1. **Silent Consonants Eliminated**: Consonants *c* and *p* not pronounced in standard speech are removed:
   - *acção* $\to$ *ação*, *óptimo* $\to$ *ótimo*, *direcção* $\to$ *direção*.
2. **Trema Abolished**: The trema (¨) was completely eliminated in native words (*cinqüenta* $\to$ *cinquenta*, *lingüiça* $\to$ *linguiça*).
3. **Diphthong Paroxytone Accents Removed**: Paroxytone words with open diphthongs *ei* and *oi* lost their acute accents (*idéia* $\to$ *ideia*, *jibóia* $\to$ *jiboia*).
4. **Hyphenation Harmonization**: Prefix rules where prefix ends in a vowel and root starts with same vowel (*micro-ondas*, *anti-inflamatório*).

## 3. Vowel System & Nasalization
Portuguese possesses one of the richest vowel inventories in the Romance family:
- **Oral Vowels**: 7 to 9 vowel phonemes (/i, e, ɛ, a, ɐ, ɔ, o, u, ɨ/).
  - High degree of unstressed vowel reduction in European Portuguese (/e/ $\to$ [ɨ], /o/ $\to$ [u], /a/ $\to$ [ɐ]).
- **Nasal Vowels**: 5 pure nasal phonemes:
  - /ĩ/ (*sim, cinto*), /ẽ/ (*vento, dente*), /ɐ̃/ (*canta, lã*), /õ/ (*ponte, bom*), /ũ/ (*mundo, um*).
- **Nasal Diphthongs**: /ɐ̃w̃/ (*mão*), /õj̃/ (*põe*), /ɐ̃j̃/ (*mãe*), /ẽj̃/ (*bem*).

## 4. Consonantal System & Palatalization
- **Sibilants & Coda S**:
  - In European Portuguese and Rio de Janeiro, syllable-final *s* is postalveolar [ʃ] before voiceless consonants or pause, and [ʒ] before voiced consonants (*os livros* [uʒ livruʃ]).
  - In São Paulo and Southern Brazil, coda *s* remains alveolar [s] and [z].
- **Affrication of Dental Stops (Brazilian Portuguese)**:
  - /t/ and /d/ before /i/ or unstressed word-final /e/ are affricated to [tʃ] and [dʒ] (*dia* [dʒi.ɐ], *noite* [noj.tʃi]).
- **Rhotics**:
  - Alveolar tap [ɾ] (*caro, prato*).
  - Strong rhotic (word-initial *r-*, *-rr-*, or coda): Uvular trill/fricative [ʁ / χ] in Portugal and Rio, glottal fricative [h] in much of Brazil.
