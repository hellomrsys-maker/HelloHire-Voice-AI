# French Morphological Analysis & Inflection Rules (Word Layer)

## 1. Verbal Conjugation Groups
French verbs are traditionally categorized into three morphological groups:
1. **1st Group (-er verbs)**: ~89% of verbs (*parler, aimer, chanter*). Regular stem + endings:
   - Present: *-e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent*
   - Imparfait: *-ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient*
   - Futur simple: *-erai, -eras, -era, -erons, -erez, -eront*
   - Subjonctif présent: *-e, -es, -e, -ions, -iez, -ent*
   - Participe passé: *-é*
   - Exception: *aller* (3rd group irregular).
2. **2nd Group (-ir with -iss- infix)**: (*finir, choisir, grandir*).
   - Present: *-is, -is, -it, -issons, -issez, -issent*
   - Participe passé: *-i*
3. **3rd Group (Irregular verbs)**:
   - -re (*vendre, attendre, prendre, mettre, faire, dire*)
   - -oir (*pouvoir, vouloir, devoir, savoir, voir, recevoir*)
   - -ir without -iss- (*venir, partir, dormir, sortir*)

## 2. Compound Tense Auxiliary Selection: *Être* vs. *Avoir*
All French compound tenses (Passé Composé, Plus-que-parfait, Futur Antérieur, Conditionnel Passé, Subjonctif Passé) require an auxiliary (*être* or *avoir*) + past participle.

### Verbs taking *Être*:
1. **The 14 Motion & State Verbs (DR MRS VANDERTRAMP)**:
   - *Devenir, Revenir, Monter, Rester, Sortir, Venir, Aller, Naître, Descendre, Entrer, Rentrer, Tomber, Retourner, Arriver, Mourir, Partir*.
2. **All Reflexive / Pronominal Verbs**:
   - *Se laver, se souvenir, se réveiller, s'asseoir, se tromper*.

### All other transitive and intransitive verbs take *Avoir*.

## 3. Past Participle Agreement Rules (*L'Accord du Participe Passé*)
1. **Auxiliary *Être***:
   - The past participle ALWAYS agrees in gender and number with the subject:
     - *Il est allé* (masc sing)
     - *Elle est allée* (fem sing: +e)
     - *Ils sont allés* (masc plur: +s)
     - *Elles sont allées* (fem plur: +es)
2. **Auxiliary *Avoir***:
   - The past participle NEVER agrees with the subject.
   - It agrees in gender and number with the direct object (**COD**) IF AND ONLY IF the direct object precedes the auxiliary:
     - *J'ai mangé les pommes.* (COD *les pommes* is after verb -> no agreement)
     - *Les pommes que j'ai mangées.* (COD *les pommes* precedes -> fem plur agreement: +es)
     - *Je les ai vues.* (COD *les* refers to females and precedes -> fem plur: +es)

## 4. Nominal and Adjectival Inflection
- **Gender Formation**: Masculine base -> Feminine typically +e (*grand -> grande*, *chanteur -> chanteuse*, *acteur -> actrice*, *bon -> bonne*).
- **Plural Formation**: Singular base -> Plural typically +s (*homme -> hommes*), -al -> -aux (*cheval -> chevaux*), -au/-eau/-eu -> -x (*château -> châteaux*).
