# French Pragmatics, Sociolinguistics & Register (Use Layer)

## 1. The T-V Distinction (*Tutoiement* vs. *Vouvoiement*)
The choice between the second-person singular informal pronoun *tu* and the formal/deferential/plural pronoun *vous* is the fundamental pragmatic parameter of French social interaction:

### Tier 1: Informal / Intimate (*Tutoiement* - *Tu*)
- Family members, romantic partners, close friends, fellow students, young children, peers in casual online contexts.
- Uses 2nd person singular verb inflections: *Tu viens ?*, *Prends ton temps.*

### Tier 2: Formal / Professional (*Vouvoiement de politesse* - *Vous*)
- Superiors, clients, strangers, adult colleagues, service personnel, elders.
- Uses 2nd person plural verb inflections with singular semantic reference: *Vous désirez, Madame ?*
- In written professional and administrative communication, *vouvoiement* is non-negotiable.

### Tier 3: Plural Collective (*Vouvoiement pluriel* - *Vous*)
- Group addressing regardless of personal intimacy level: *Vous venez tous demain ?*

## 2. Epistolary Formulas & Correspondence Architecture

### Salutations:
- Formal / Administrative: *Madame, Monsieur,*, *Monsieur le Directeur,*, *Madame la Présidente,*
- Semi-formal: *Chère collègue,*, *Cher Monsieur Dupont,*
- Informal: *Cher Thomas,*, *Chère Claire,*, *Bonjour à tous,*

### Standard Formal Closing Formulas (*Formules de politesse*):
1. **High Administrative / Official**:
   - *Je vous prie d'agréer, Madame, Monsieur, l'expression de mes salutations distinguées.*
   - *Veuillez agréer, Monsieur le Directeur, l'assurance de ma parfaite considération.*
2. **Standard Professional**:
   - *Cordialement,*
   - *Bien cordialement,*
   - *Sincères salutations,*
3. **Informal**:
   - *Amitiés,*
   - *Bises,*, *À très bientôt,*
