# French Phonological & Orthographic Architecture (Text-Sound Layer)

## 1. Phonological Inventory
- **Oral Vowels**: /i, e, ɛ, a, ɑ, ɔ, o, u, y, ø, œ, ə/
- **Nasal Vowels**:
  - /ɑ̃/ (*an, am, en, em*)
  - /ɛ̃/ (*in, im, ain, aim, ein, yn, ym*)
  - /ɔ̃/ (*on, om*)
  - /œ̃/ (*un, um* - in modern standard Metropolitan French often merging with /ɛ̃/)
- **Semi-Vowels (Glides)**: /j, w, ɥ/ (*hier, oui, huit*)
- **Consonants**: /p, b, t, d, k, g, f, v, s, z, ʃ, ʒ, m, n, ɲ, ŋ, ʁ, l/

## 2. Elision (*L'Élision*)
Elision is the suppression of a final unstressed vowel (*e*, *a*, or *i* in *si + il*) before an initial vowel or silent *h* (*h muet*), replaced orthographically by an apostrophe:
- *le + ami* -> *l'ami*
- *la + eau* -> *l'eau*
- *de + accord* -> *d'accord*
- *je + aime* -> *j'aime*
- *ce + est* -> *c'est*
- *ne + a* -> *n'a*
- *que + il* -> *qu'il*
- *si + il* -> *s'il* (only before *il* / *ils*, never before *elle* -> *si elle*)

### Exception: *H Aspiré* (Aspirated H)
Certain Germanic/loan words starting with *h* are designated *h aspiré*. They do NOT permit elision or liaison:
- *le héros* (never *l'héros*)
- *la hauteur* (never *l'hauteur*)
- *les haricots* [le aʁiko] (no liaison /z/)
- *la honte*, *le hasard*, *le homard*, *le hérisson*.

In contrast, Latinate *h muet* requires elision and liaison:
- *l'homme* (elision)
- *les hommes* [lezɔm] (liaison with /z/)
- *l'histoire*, *l'hôpital*, *l'heure*.

## 3. Liaison Typology
Liaison is the pronunciation of an otherwise silent latent word-final consonant before a word beginning with a vowel or silent *h*:
1. **Compulsory Liaisons**:
   - Determiner + Noun/Adjective (*les_amis* [lezami], *un_homme* [œ̃nɔm])
   - Subject Clitic + Verb (*ils_ont* [ilzɔ̃], *on_arrive* [ɔ̃naʁiv])
   - Monosyllabic Preposition + Noun (*dans_un* [dɑ̃zœ̃], *en_effet* [ɑ̃nefɛ])
   - Preposed Adjective + Noun (*grand_arbre* [gʁɑ̃taʁbʁ], *beaux_yeux* [bozjø])
2. **Forbidden Liaisons**:
   - After *et* (ALWAYS forbidden: *et un ami* -> [e œ̃nami], never [etœ̃nami])
   - Singular Noun + Following Word (*un chat intelligent* -> [œ̃ ʃa ɛ̃tɛliʒɑ̃], no /t/)
   - Before *h aspiré* (*les héros* -> [le eʁo])
   - After inverted subject pronoun (*sont-ils arrivés* -> no liaison after *ils*).

## 4. Orthographic Diacritics
- Accent aigu (`´`): *é* (/e/)
- Accent grave (``` ` ```): *à, è, ù* (/ɛ/ for è; distinguishes homophones *a/à*, *ou/où*, *la/là*)
- Accent circonflexe (`^`): *â, ê, î, ô, û* (historical s-deletion: *forêt* < *forest*, *hôpital* < *hospital*)
- Tréma (`¨`): *ë, ï, ü* (diphthong hiatus separation: *Noël, maïs, ambiguë*)
- Cédille (`¸`): *ç* (forces soft /s/ before *a, o, u*: *français, garçon, reçu*).
