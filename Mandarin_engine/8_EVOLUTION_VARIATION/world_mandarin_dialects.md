# Mandarin Engine — Layer 8: Evolution & Dialectal Variation
## World Chinese Varieties & Diachronic Evolution

### 1. Major Pluricentric Standards
- **Mainland China Standard (普通话 / Pǔtōnghuà)**: Based on Beijing phonology and Northern syntax; written in Simplified Chinese Characters (`规范汉字`).
- **Taiwan Standard (国语 / Guóyǔ)**: Preserves Traditional Chinese characters (`正体字`), distinctive lexical preferences, and absence of heavy northern Erhua (`儿化`).
- **Singapore / Malaysian Chinese (华语 / Huáyǔ)**: Influenced by Southern Min (Hokkien), Cantonese, and Malay contact loans.

### 2. Digital Registers & Neologisms
- Internet Slang: `点赞` (give like), `打call` (cheer on), `躺平` (lie flat), `内卷` (involution / rat race), `666` (smooth / impressive), `吃瓜群众` (onlookers eating watermelon).
- Western & Japanese Loanwords: `吉他` (guitar), `咖啡` (coffee), `沙发` (sofa), `幽默` (humor - coined by Lin Yutang), `人气` (popularity - from Japanese).
