# Mandarin Engine — Layer 5: Pragmatic (Use Layer)
## Chinese Face (面子 / 脸) Dynamics, Indirectness & Honorific Pragmatics

### 1. The Dual Concepts of Face: Mianzi (面子) vs Lian (脸)
1. **Lian (脸)**: Moral integrity, character, decency. To "lose face" (丢脸 / diūliǎn) signifies moral failure or public shame.
2. **Mianzi (面子)**: Social prestige, reputation, honor, vanity. "Giving face" (给面子 / gěi miànzi) strengthens collaborative social bonds (*guanxi*).

### 2. Pragmatics of Indirectness & Hedging
Direct refusals (`不行` / `我拒绝`) cause severe loss of face in formal/business interaction. Mandatory polite strategies:
- **Temporal Postponement**: `我们再研究研究` (We will study it further), `过两天再说` (Let's talk in a couple of days).
- **Inconvenience Euphemism**: `这事儿可能有点儿不太方便` (This matter might be slightly inconvenient).
- **Diffident Modesty (谦逊语)**: `哪里哪里` (Where where / not at all), `过奖了` (You overpraise me), `抛砖引玉` (Casting a brick to attract jade / offering humble opening thoughts).

### 3. Professional & Social Honorifics
- Pronoun of Deference: `您` (nín - polite second person).
- Inquiring Surname: `您贵姓？` (May I ask your honorable surname?).
- Formal titles: Surname + Position (e.g. `李总` General Manager Li, `张教授` Professor Zhang, `王主任` Director Wang, `陈老师` Teacher Chen).
- Deferential openers in correspondence: `敬启者` (To whom it may concern), `祝好` (Best wishes), `顺祝商祺` (Wishing you business prosperity).
