# Mandarin Engine — Layer 2: Morphological Analysis (Word Layer)
## Chinese Isolating Morphology, Aspect Particles & Classifier Compounding

### 1. Absolute Lack of Inflectional Conjugation
Mandarin verbs and adjectives exhibit **0 inflectional variance**:
- No agreement for person, number, or gender.
- No morphological tense suffixes (past/present/future).
- Aspect and temporal relationships are borne exclusively by lexical adverbs and grammatical particles.

### 2. The Triad of Aspect Particles
1. **Perfective Particle 了 (le - 动词后体标记)**:
   - Marks completion/termination of an action within a bounded frame.
   - Distinct from sentence-final Modal Particle 了 (le - 语气助词, marking new situation/change of state).
   - 例: 我买了一本书。(I bought a book.) vs 下雨了。(It has started raining.)
2. **Durative Particle 着 (zhe - 伴随/持续体)**:
   - Marks continuous state or accompanying background action.
   - 例: 门开着。(The door is open.)
   - 例: 他笑着说话。(He speaks smiling.)
3. **Experiential Particle 过 (guo - 经历体)**:
   - Marks that an action has been experienced at least once in the past.
   - 例: 我去过中国。(I have been to China before.)

### 3. Nominal Classifiers (量词 - Measure Words)
Every count noun requires an obligatory shape-based or semantic classifier when quantified by a numeral or demonstrative:
- `个` (gè): General default classifier (人, 苹果, 问题).
- `条` (tiáo): Long, flexible, or elongated objects (鱼, 路, 裤子, 绳子).
- `张` (zhāng): Flat sheets, surfaces, or face-like items (纸, 桌子, 照片, 票).
- `本` (běn): Bound volumes (书, 词典, 笔记本).
- `支` (zhī): Stick-like or slender rigid items (笔, 枪, 箭).
- `只` (zhī): Small animals, single of a pair (猫, 鸟, 手, 眼睛).
- `辆` (liàng): Wheeled vehicles (车, 自行车).
- `台` (tái): Machinery, appliances, computers (电脑, 电视).
- `栋 / 座` (dòng / zuò): Buildings, large stationary landforms (楼, 房子, 山).
