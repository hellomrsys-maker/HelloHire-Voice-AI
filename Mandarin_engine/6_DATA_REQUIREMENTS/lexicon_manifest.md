# Mandarin Engine — Layer 6: Data Requirements
## Lexicon, Corpora Manifest & Grammatical Databases

### 1. Corpora Specifications
- **Penn Chinese Treebank (CTB)**: Morphosyntactic constituency & dependency trees.
- **Chinese Universal Dependencies (UD Chinese-GSD / PUD)**: Universal dependency annotations.
- **LDC Chinese Gigaword**: Broad-coverage modern journalistic and formal Chinese.
- **Beijing Language and Culture University (BLCU) HSK Corpus**: Graded learner syntax and error taxonomy.

### 2. Dictionaries & Lexical Databases
- **CC-CEDICT**: 120,000+ entry Community Chinese-English dictionary with Pinyin and Hanzi variants.
- **Modern Chinese Classifier Database (`measure_word_db.json`)**: Matrix of 150+ classifiers linked to semantic noun categories.
- **Complete Chengyu Dictionary (`chengyu_db.json`)**: 20,000+ four-character idioms with historical citations and figurative meanings.
- **Standard HSK Vocabulary Tier (HSK 1-9 3.0 Standard)**: Graded frequencies for education and evaluation.
