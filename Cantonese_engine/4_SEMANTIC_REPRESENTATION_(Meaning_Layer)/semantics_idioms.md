# Cantonese Semantic Representation & Idiomatic Space (Meaning Layer)

## 1. Numeral & Demonstrative Classifiers (量詞)
Cantonese employs an intricate inventory of shape, function, and category classifiers:

| Classifier | Jyutping | Semantic Category | Typical Nouns |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **個** | *go3* | General default, human beings, abstract concepts | *人, 蘋果, 問題, 地方* |
| **隻** | *zek3* | Animals, single items of pairs, vessels, hands | *狗, 貓, 船, 手, 腳, 隻眼* |
| **條** | *tiu4* | Long, flexible, or cylindrical objects | *路, 魚, 褲, 繩, 命* |
| **件** | *gin6* | Articles of clothing, events, luggages | *衫, 事, 行李* |
| **本** | *bun2* | Bound printed books and registers | *書, 雜誌, 字典* |
| **架** | *gaa3* | Vehicles, machinery, airplanes | *車, 飛機, 電腦* |
| **間** | *gaan1* | Rooms, buildings, shops, institutions | *屋, 房, 學校, 公司, 舖頭* |
| **張** | *zoeng1* | Flat planar surfaces, paper, tables, chairs | *紙, 枱, 飛 (ticket), 相* |
| **啲** | *di1* | Plurality ("the / some"), mass entities | *書 (the books), 嘢食, 水* |

## 2. Vivid Everyday Idioms & Slang (廣東話俗語與歇後語)
Cantonese culture features an extraordinarily creative colloquial lexicon rooted in trade, cuisine, and theater:
- *飲茶* (*jam2 caa4* = drink tea / go for dim sum $\to$ social gathering)
- *炒魷魚* (*caau2 jau4 jyu2* = stir-fry squid $\to$ get fired / sacked)
- *食死貓* (*sik6 sei2 maau1* = eat a dead cat $\to$ take the blame for someone else)
- *賣豬仔* (*maai6 zyu1 zai2* = sell piglet $\to$ deceive someone into labor / betray)
- *放飛機* (*fong3 fei1 gei1* = release paper airplane $\to$ stand someone up / break a promise)
- *食檸檬* (*sik6 ning4 mung1* = eat a lemon $\to$ get rejected in romance)

## 3. Xiehouyu (歇後語 - Two-Part Allegorical Sayings)
- *泥水佬開門 —— 過得自己過得人* (*nai4 seoi2 lou2 hoi1 mun4: gwo3 dak1 zi6 gei2 gwo3 dak1 jan4* = "A mason opening a door: good enough for oneself, good enough for others $\to$ satisfactory standard").
- *阿崩叫狗 —— 越叫越走* (*aa3 bang1 giu3 gau2: jyut6 giu3 jyut6 zau2* = "A hare-lip calling a dog: the more he calls, the faster it runs away").
