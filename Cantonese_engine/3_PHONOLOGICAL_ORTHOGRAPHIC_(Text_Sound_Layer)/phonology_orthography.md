# Cantonese Phonology & Jyutping Orthography Specifications (Text/Sound Layer)

## 1. The Nine Phonemic Tones (九聲六調)
Standard Cantonese (Guangzhou / Hong Kong) possesses nine phonemic tones historically derived from the four Middle Chinese tone registers split by consonant voicing (Yin/Yang). Because the three entering tones (*入聲*) share the pitch contours of three open tones, they are analyzed as 6 contrastive pitch contours plus 3 checked tones:

| Tone # | Traditional Name | Pitch Value | Category | Coda Type | Jyutping Number | Example | Gloss |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | 陰平 (High Level) | 55 / 53 | Citation | Open / Nasal | 1 | 詩 (*si1*) | poem |
| 2 | 陰上 (High Rising) | 25 | Citation | Open / Nasal | 2 | 史 (*si2*) | history |
| 3 | 陰去 (Mid Level) | 33 | Citation | Open / Nasal | 3 | 試 (*si3*) | test |
| 4 | 陽平 (Low Falling) | 21 | Citation | Open / Nasal | 4 | 時 (*si4*) | time |
| 5 | 陽上 (Low Rising) | 23 | Citation | Open / Nasal | 5 | 市 (*si5*) | market |
| 6 | 陽去 (Low Level) | 22 | Citation | Open / Nasal | 6 | 事 (*si6*) | matter |
| 7 | 上陰入 (High Checked) | 5 | Entering | Stop (-p, -t, -k) | 1 / 7 | 識 (*sik1*) | know |
| 8 | 下陰入 (Mid Checked) | 3 | Entering | Stop (-p, -t, -k) | 3 / 8 | 錫 (*sek3*) | kiss / tin |
| 9 | 陽入 (Low Checked) | 2 | Entering | Stop (-p, -t, -k) | 6 / 9 | 食 (*sik6*) | eat |

## 2. Syllable Structure & Unreleased Stop Codas
Every Cantonese syllable conforms to:
$$\text{Syllable} = (C_1) + [V] + (C_2) + \text{Tone}$$
- **Consonant Onset ($C_1$)**: 19 consonants: *b, p, m, f, d, t, n, l, g, k, ng, h, gw, kw, w, z, c, s, j*.
- **Nucleus ($V$)**: 8 vowels (*aa, a, e, i, o, u, oe, yu*).
- **Consonant Coda ($C_2$)**:
  - Nasals: *-m, -n, -ng* (e.g. *sam1, san1, sang1*).
  - Unreleased checked stops: **-p, -t, -k** (e.g. *sap6, sat1, sak1*).
  - Syllabic nasals: *m4* (唔) and *ng5* (五).

## 3. Written Cantonese Orthography & HKSCS
Cantonese is written in Traditional Chinese characters supplemented by the **Hong Kong Supplementary Character Set (HKSCS)** for indigenous spoken morphemes:
- **口-radical innovations**: *唔, 嘅, 喺, 咗, 哋, 咁, 噉, 乜, 嘢, 啫, 喇, 喎, 㗎, 喎, 嗱*.
- Romanization Standard: **LSHK Jyutping** (The Linguistic Society of Hong Kong Cantonese Romanization Scheme).
