# Cantonese Pragmatics, Discourse Particles & Register (Use Layer)

## 1. Sentence-Final Particles (SFPs / 句末助詞)
Cantonese is world-renowned for its dense, multifaceted system of Sentence-Final Particles (>30 distinct particles and clusters). SFPs convey evidentiality, emotional stance, commitment, intimacy, and epistemic modality:

| SFP | Tone | Discourse Function | Example | Gloss / Nuance |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **喇** | *laa1 / laa3* | Realized change of state / Urgent suggestion | *我走喇* | I'm leaving now! |
| **呀** | *aa3 / aa4* | Softening / Seeking confirmation / Question | *係呀* | Yes, that's right. |
| **㗎** | *gaa3 / gaa4* | Expressing established assertion or strong surprise | *真係㗎？* | Really?! |
| **啫** | *ze1* | Diminishing / "Only / merely / no big deal" | *玩吓啫* | Just playing, that's all. |
| **喎** | *wo3 / wo4* | Reported speech / Quotative evidential | *佢話聽日嚟喎* | He said he's coming tomorrow (reportedly). |
| **囉** | *lo1* | Obviousness / "As you know / naturally" | *係噉囉* | That's how it is, obviously. |
| **添** | *tim1* | Additional / Surprise excess ("even / more") | *食咗兩碗添* | Even ate two bowls! |
| **咩** | *me1* | Rhetorical doubt / Skeptical inquiry | *係咩？* | Is that so? (I doubt it). |
| **咋** | *zaa3* | Exclusivity limit ("only / just that much") | *五蚊咋* | Only five dollars! |

## 2. Diglossia & Register Strata (白話 vs 文言 / 雙層語言)
Cantonese speakers navigate a sharp diglossic boundary:
1. **High Formal / Written Standard (書面語 - Syu1 Min6 Jyu5)**:
   - Used in government gazettes, news broadcasts, legal contracts, and corporate emails.
   - Grammatically matches Modern Standard Chinese with Cantonese pronunciation (*我們, 不, 是, 吃, 的*).
2. **Spoken Colloquial / Vernacular (口語 / 白話 - Hau2 Jyu5)**:
   - Used in daily conversation, social media, comic books, courtroom testimony, and pop lyrics.
   - Employs authentic Cantonese syntax and indigenous morphemes (*我哋, 唔, 係, 食, 嘅*).

## 3. Epistolary Standards
- Formal Commercial: *逕啟者 / 敬啟者：... 此致 貴公司 / 祝 商祺*
- Friendly / Vernacular Email: *Hi [Name], 麻煩你幫手睇睇呢份文件，唔該晒！* (Please help take a look at this document, thank you so much!)
