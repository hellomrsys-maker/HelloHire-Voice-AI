# Cantonese Morphological Analysis & Aspectual Suffixes (Word Layer)

## 1. Postverbal Aspect Markers (體貌助詞)
Cantonese possesses an exceptionally rich inventory of postverbal aspectual enclitics that attach directly to the verbal root:

| Particle | Jyutping | Aspectual Meaning | Example | Gloss |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **咗** | *zo2* | Perfective (completed action) | *食咗飯* | have eaten |
| **緊** | *gan2* | Progressive (ongoing in real time) | *睇緊書* | reading a book |
| **過** | *gwo3* | Experiential (past life experience) | *去過日本* | have been to Japan |
| **開** | *hoi1* | Habituative (ongoing customary practice) | *食開呢間* | accustomed to eating at this place |
| **住** | *zyu6* | Continuous / Durative state | *着住件衫* | wearing a shirt |
| **吓** | *haa5* | Delimitative / Tentative (brief action) | *試吓* | try it out a bit |
| **晒** | *saai3* | Completive / Quantificational ("all / completely") | *賣晒* | completely sold out |
| **埋** | *maai4* | Inclusive ("including / also") | *食埋佢* | finish eating it up too |
| **翻** | *faan1* | Restorative ("back to original state") | *買翻* | buy back / return to |

## 2. Pronominal System & Plural Marker (哋)
Personal pronouns append the plural enclitic **哋** (*dei6*):
- 1st Person: **我** (*ngo5* - I/me) $\to$ **我哋** (*ngo5 dei6* - we/us)
- 2nd Person: **你** (*nei5* - you) $\to$ **你哋** (*nei5 dei6* - you plural)
- 3rd Person: **佢** (*keoi5* - he/she/it) $\to$ **佢哋** (*keoi5 dei6* - they/them)

## 3. Cantonese Indigenous Morphemes (粵語固有詞)
Cantonese retains Old and Middle Chinese lexical roots as well as indigenous Tai-Kadai substrate morphemes:
- *食* (*sik6* - to eat; cf. Mandarin *吃*)
- *飲* (*jam2* - to drink; cf. Mandarin *喝*)
- *睇* (*tai2* - to look / watch; cf. Mandarin *看*)
- *講* (*gong2* - to speak / say; cf. Mandarin *說*)
- *諗* (*nam2* - to think; cf. Mandarin *想*)
- *喺* (*hai2* - to be located at; cf. Mandarin *在*)
- *係* (*hai6* - copula to be; cf. Mandarin *是*)
- *乜* (*mat1* - what; cf. Mandarin *什麼*)
- *嘢* (*je5* - thing / matter; cf. Mandarin *東西*)
- *邊度* (*bin1 dou6* - where; cf. Mandarin *哪裡*)
- *點解* (*dim2 gaai2* - why; cf. Mandarin *為什麼*)

## 4. Morphological Reduplication
1. **Adjective Reduplication (AABB / ABB)**:
   - *乾乾淨淨* (*gon1 gon1 zeng6 zeng6* = spotlessly clean)
   - *紅卜卜* (*hung4 buk1 buk1* = rosy red)
   - *黃黚黚* (*wong4 kam4 kam4* = deep yellow)
2. **Classifier Reduplication (Every / Each)**:
   - *個個* (*go3 go3* = everyone)
   - *隻隻* (*zek3 zek3* = every single animal/item)
