# Layer 8: Evolution & Variation (World Italian & Dialects) — Italian Language Engine

## 1. Standard Italian & the Language-Dialect Continuum
Italian presents a unique sociolinguistic landscape characterized by diglossia and dilalia between:
1. **Italiano Standard / Neo-Standard**:
   - Standard literary language originating from the 14th-century Florentine triumvirate (Dante, Petrarca, Boccaccio), codified by Pietro Bembo in 1525 and Alessandro Manzoni in the 19th century.
   - Modern Neo-Standard features: simplification of *egli/ella* to *lui/lei*, generalized *gli* for plural/feminine indirect object, use of indicative in place of subjunctive in informal speech.
2. **Regional Italian (Italiano Regionale)**:
   - Italian spoken with regional phonetic, lexical, and syntactic substrate influences:
     - **Northern Italian (Settentrionale)**: Absence of *passato remoto* (replaced universally by *passato prossimo*), omission of double negation with *mica*, use of definite article before female given names (*la Giulia*), avoidance of syntactic doubling.
     - **Central Italian (Toscano / Romano)**: Retention of *passato remoto*, gorgia toscana (spirantization of intervocalic stops /k, t, p/ $\to$ [h, θ, ɸ]), affrication of /s/ after liquids/nasals (*il penziero*), use of *noi si va* for *andiamo*.
     - **Southern Italian (Meridionale)**: Strong preservation of *passato remoto*, accusative prepositional marking with *a* (personal *a*: *chiama a Maria*), raddoppiamento fonosintattico, possessive enclisis (*mamma mia*, *fratemo*).
3. **Regional Languages / Dialects (Dialetti Italiani)**:
   - Independent Romance languages descended directly from Vulgar Latin (not corruptions of Italian):
     - Gallo-Italic (Piedmontese, Lombard, Ligurian, Emilian-Romagnol).
     - Venetian.
     - Central Italian (Romanesco, Umbrian, Marchigiano).
     - Upper Southern (Neapolitan, Campanian, Lucanian, Pugliese).
     - Extreme Southern (Sicilian, Calabrian, Salentino).
     - Non-Italo-Romance minority languages: Sardinian, Friulian, Ladin, Occitan, Franco-Provençal, Arbëresh (Albanian), Griko (Greek).

## 2. World Italian (Italiano all'Estero)
- **Switzerland**: Official language in Canton Ticino and Grigioni; specific lexical Helvetisms (*azione* = sale/discount, *notaio* = notary).
- **Emigration Diasporas**: Historic Italian communities in the Americas (Argentina - *Cocoliche*, Brazil - *Talian*, USA - Italian-American contact vernaculars), Australia, and Canada.
