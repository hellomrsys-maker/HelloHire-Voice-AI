# Layer 5: Pragmatic (Use Layer) — Italian Language Engine

## 1. Social Deixis & Address System (Sistema di Allocuzione)
Italian maintains a three-tiered register hierarchy governed by social distance, age, and professional deference:

### 1.1 Informal Address (Dare del Tu)
- Pronoun: *tu*, oblique clitic *ti*, tonic *te*, possessive *tuo/tua*.
- Verb: 2nd person singular (*Come ti chiami? Dove vai?*).
- Usage: Family members, close friends, colleagues of equal status, children, and peer interactions among youth.

### 1.2 Formal Address (Dare del Lei)
- Pronoun: *Lei* (grammatically 3rd person feminine singular, regardless of the addressee's biological gender).
- Oblique clitic: *La* (direct object), *Le* (indirect object).
- Possessive: *Suo / Sua / Suoi / Sue* (often capitalized in formal correspondence).
- Verb: 3rd person singular (*Come si chiama? Vuole accomodarsi? Ha bisogno di aiuto?*).
- Usage: Strangers, superiors, professors, professionals, doctors, clients.

### 1.3 Plural Formal Address (Dare del Voi / Loro)
- Standard Formal Plural: *Voi* (verb 2nd person plural) is widely used in modern spoken and corporate Italian (*Come state? Volete accomodarvi?*).
- Ultra-Formal / Bureaucratic: *Loro* (verb 3rd person plural) (*Accomodino pure, signori*), primarily in high diplomacy and luxury hospitality.

## 2. Epistolary Standards (Corrispondenza Formale)
Italian business and institutional letters follow rigid opening and closing formulas:
- **Salutation**:
  - *Gentile Dottore / Dottoressa [Cognome],*
  - *Egregio Signore / Gentile Signora,*
  - *Chiarissimo Professore [Cognome],* (academic)
- **Opening**:
  - *Le scrivo in merito alla Sua richiesta...*
  - *Con la presente desideriamo informarLa che...*
- **Closing**:
  - *In attesa di un Suo cortese riscontro, Le porgo i miei più cordiali saluti.*
  - *Distinti saluti,*
  - *Cordiali saluti,*

## 3. Discourse Markers & Conversational Modulators
- **Allora**: Temporal sequencer or discursive transition ("so, well then").
- **Mica**: Polar negative presupposition marker: *Non è mica facile!* (It's not that easy at all!).
- **Magari**: Optative longing ("if only!") or hypothetical possibility ("maybe / perhaps").
- **Figurati / Si figuri**: Modest dismissal of thanks ("don't mention it! / you're welcome!").
