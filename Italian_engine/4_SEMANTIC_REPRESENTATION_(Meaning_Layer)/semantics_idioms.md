# Layer 4: Semantic Representation (Meaning Layer) — Italian Language Engine

## 1. Copula Semantics & Aspectual Predicates
Unlike Spanish or Portuguese (*ser/estar* split), standard Italian uses a single copula *essere* for both permanent qualities and temporary states, but encodes aspectual differentiation via lexical and constructional predicates:
- Permanent: *Marco è simpatico / è italiano.*
- Temporary State: *Marco è stanco / è a Roma.*
- Progressive Aspect: Auxiliary *stare* + gerundio (*Marco sta parlando* = Marco is speaking).
- Imminent Aspect: *stare per* + infinitive (*Il treno sta per partire* = The train is about to depart).

## 2. Modal Verbs & Semantic Nuances (Dovere, Potere, Volere, Sapere)
- **Dovere**:
  - Epistemic necessity ("must / surely"): *Deve essere arrivato* (He must have arrived).
  - Deontic obligation ("must / to have to"): *Devo finire il lavoro* (I must finish the work).
  - Destiny / Future in the past: *Doveva partire il giorno dopo* (He was destined to leave the next day).
- **Potere vs Sapere**:
  - *Potere*: Possibility or permission (*Posso entrare?* = May I enter? *Posso correre* = I am physically able).
  - *Sapere*: Acquired cognitive or practical skill (*So nuotare* = I know how to swim).

## 3. Prepositional Semantic Core
- **Da**:
  - Origin / Source: *Vengo da Milano.*
  - Duration up to present: *Vivo qui da tre anni* (I have been living here for three years).
  - Purpose / Agentive: *occhiali da sole* (sunglasses), *scritto da Dante* (written by Dante).
  - Residence/Office of someone: *Vado dal medico / da Marco* (going to the doctor's / Marco's place).
- **In vs A (Geographic Governance)**:
  - Cities and small islands take `a`: *a Roma, a Napoli, a Capri*.
  - Countries, regions, continents, and large islands take `in`: *in Italia, in Toscana, in Europa, in Sicilia*.
  - Means of transport: *in treno, in macchina, in aereo*.

## 4. Classical Rhetorical Idioms & Collocations
- *In bocca al lupo!* $\to$ *Crepi!* (Break a leg! $\to$ May the wolf die!).
- *Prendere lucciole per lanterne* (To mistake fireflies for lanterns / to be completely mistaken).
- *Non vedere l'ora* (Cannot wait / looking forward eagerly).
- *Fare fiasco* (To fail completely).
- *Rompere il ghiaccio* (To break the ice).
